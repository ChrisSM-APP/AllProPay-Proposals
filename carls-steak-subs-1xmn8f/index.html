What We Found

1. Your markup is roughly 2x to 3x the market rate for a restaurant your size

You are on interchange-plus, which is the right pricing model. But the markup on top of interchange is where the leak is. At your volume and average ticket, the typical AllProPay markup on a card-present restaurant lands in the 0.35% to 0.60% range. You are at 1.25%.

On $30k/month, the markup gap alone is roughly $195 to $270 per month, before any monthly fees, statement fees, or junk-line items.

2. Deposit delays and customer service are processor problems, not pricing problems

You flagged two things that have nothing to do with rate: deposits showing up inconsistently, and customer service that isn't there when you need it. Both are common with large national platforms because your file is one of millions. With AllProPay you get next-day funding standard, batch by 8pm Eastern, and a direct line to a real person who knows your account.

For a $30k/month restaurant, every day of delayed funding is roughly $1,000 sitting out of your bank account. Reliable next-day deposits matter as much as the rate.

3. The Clover hardware is worth keeping, but it locks you to FirstData

Clover is owned by Fiserv, the same company as FirstData. The hardware is good, but the device is tied to their processing platform. To switch processors and keep the experience Carl likes, there are two clean paths:

  • Path A - Reprogram the Clover. AllProPay can move the Clover Terminal and Duo Station to our Fiserv-aligned platform, subject to device eligibility. Same hardware, same staff workflow, new processor and pricing underneath.
  • Path B - Replace with EPOSnow or Kwick. Restaurant-grade POS systems built for sub shops and quick-service. Better support, lower long-term cost, and built-in online ordering. Best long-term move if Carl is open to a hardware swap.

Website integration works under either path through Authorize.Net or CardPointe, so the online sales settle into the same deposit cycle.
