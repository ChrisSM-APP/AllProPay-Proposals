Backed and flexible

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are growing, so the account is built to not get in the way of that.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the card cost yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication.
  • Adding a third guy, a second truck, or a second phone taking payments costs nothing extra. The $25 is the account, not the seat.
  • Nothing is locked. If we stop earning it, you walk, and you take your customers and your history with you.
