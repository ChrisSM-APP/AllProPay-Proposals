Where We Are

You told us the goal on the call: keep the setup you already run, the Edge POS and Verifone terminals on Sola, in the store and online, and stop carrying the full credit card processing cost yourself. You also wanted to understand how surcharging actually works before deciding anything. This lays that out in plain numbers.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Your point-of-sale stays the same. You keep ringing sales in-store and online the way you do today.
  • The credit card processing cost moves off the business instead of coming out of your margin on every sale.
  • Cash, check, and debit customers keep paying the list price on the tag.
  • One flat monthly cost, clearly disclosed to card customers, and no long-term contract.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing

Same counter, same customers
Nothing changes about how a sale gets rung. Dual pricing shows a cash price and a card price, disclosed clearly at checkout, in the store and online. Your staff and your customers see exactly what applies before they pay.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

Jewelry is not a fast-food ticket. Some of your sales are big, and you should stay in control of how the fee is handled on those. The account is built for that.

  • Start with $0 card cost to the business. On a large sale where you would rather absorb the fee than pass it to the customer, you can, sale by sale.
  • If you ever decide you would rather not surcharge at all, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication. You still pay less than today, it just comes from your side instead of the customer's.
  • Everything works the same in the store and online, so there is one way to ring a sale, not two.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your cost on card sales drops toward $0. The processing fee moves to the card-paying customer, clearly disclosed at checkout.
