The Flexibility You Asked For

Today you recover card costs inside the service fee on each invoice. That is your call to make, but the card brands have standards for how fees get passed to customers, and the fines for getting it wrong land on the business. If you ever want the card fee passed automatically and compliantly, the same account does it with no new application. And if you keep bundling, a lower rate simply means the fee you recover costs you less.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today.
  • Switch to dual pricing whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run a few months on IC+, see the actual cost on your statement, and decide if you want to move it to the customer. No penalty either way.
