What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On Edge the shop posts two prices: the cash price you charge today, and a card price beside it. Cash customers pay exactly what they pay now. A customer who chooses to pay by card covers the card price, and your cost on that sale is $0. The shop deposits the cash price either way.
Simple enough for anyone at the counter
The terminal arrives already programmed with both prices. Enter the amount, the customer sees the cash price and the card price on the screen and picks one, they tap or insert, done. Nobody has to remember a percentage or do math on a ticket. I set it up with you over the phone, and if anything ever feels off you call me directly, not a ticket queue.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

When a shop takes cards the usual way, roughly 3 percent of every card sale goes to the processor and the shop eats it. That is what happened on SpotOn, and it is a fair reason to stay cash only. On Edge the card price is posted next to the cash price, the card customer covers the difference, and your cost on card sales is $0. Your only cost is a flat $25 a month for the whole account, terminal included. The $10,000 a month is an illustration, since the shop is not taking cards yet. Once you have a real month behind you I will replace it with your number. And none of this counts the customers who drive off today because they did not bring cash.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are not committing to anything today, and the account is built to fit however the shop ends up running.

  • Start when your brother starts. We open the account when you tell me to. The terminal ships programmed and ready, and the first sale is a phone call away.
  • The cash price never moves. Your regulars who pay cash see nothing change. Only the customer who chooses a card sees the card price, and they see it before they decide.
  • If you ever decide you would rather carry the fee yourself, we move you to straight interchange-plus on the same account. No reapplication, no new paperwork.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If this does not earn its place at the shop, you walk.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • The shop starts taking cards without paying for it. Your cost on every card sale is $0.
  • Nothing upfront, and one flat $25 a month covers the terminal, support, and compliance.
  • Anyone at the counter can run it on day one. Both prices are on the screen, so there is no percentage to remember and no math on the ticket.
  • An estimated $3,000 to $4,000 a year stays in the shop for every $10,000 a month in card sales, compared with paying the fees yourself.
  • Customers who do not carry cash stop being lost sales.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee.

Take a $200 ticket. A cash customer pays $200, same as today. A customer who wants to use a card sees $208 on the screen, and if they choose it, the shop still deposits the full $200 with nothing taken out. Do that on a $400 job and the card customer sees $416. Either way the shop keeps every dollar of its price, and the customer who only had a card in their wallet stays your customer. These are illustrations; the real figures come from your first month on the account.
