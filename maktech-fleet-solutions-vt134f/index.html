Where We Are

You run a fleet shop in Melissa, and most of your work gets paid through your customers' accounts payable, so cards are the smaller side of your month, under $10,000. The part that caught my attention was how you handle the fee. You told me you charge the customer for it and you put it into the cost. So your repair price already carries the processing fee, and then the fee comes out of your account anyway, through whoever sits behind the Tekmetric link. You are collecting it and paying it. That is the piece worth fixing, and it is why I wanted a few minutes with you before sending you anything.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Nothing changes about how your customers pay. Tekmetric keeps pushing the invoice, in person stays in person.
  • Your posted repair price does not have to go up, because the fee is already inside it.
  • Your fleet accounts paying through AP do not pay more than they do today.
  • One place to take a payment instead of three.
  • You are on the floor all day. Ten minutes, not an hour.
What You Get
Zero cost card processing
Here is the mechanic, and it fits what you are already doing. Your posted repair price stays the price, because it already has the fee built into it. That becomes the card price. Customers paying by check, ACH, or cash pay a lower price, and the gap between the two is the fee you no longer have to cover. The card payer pays what they pay today. You stop paying a processing fee at all, so 100% of a card payment is deposited and nothing is withdrawn at the end of the month. How much lower the check and ACH price sits is set when the account goes in, and that part is your call.
One place to take a payment
Right now you have three. Tekmetric pushes the invoice and the customer can pay on that link. If they will not, you send them a separate link out of iPOS. If they walk in, you ring it on the iPOS terminal. Two systems, three ways in, and only two of them are connected to each other. On our account it is one terminal at the front desk and one link you can text or email from the same place, so however the customer pays it lands in the same batch and the same deposit. Tekmetric keeps doing what it is good at, which is the work order and the invoice.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.
