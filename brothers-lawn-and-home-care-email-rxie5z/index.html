Where We Are

Here is what you told Kurt and me on Tuesday, so you know I was actually listening. You have run Brother's Lawn and Home Care on Jobber for four years. Scheduling, invoicing, your website and your bookkeeping all live in there, and that is exactly why you have stayed. You are paying Jobber a flat 2.7 percent on every card payment, you absorb it as a business cost rather than passing it to your clients, and in four years nobody has ever offered to lower it. That last part is the piece worth fixing.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • You keep Jobber. Scheduling, invoicing, your website and your bookkeeping do not move. You keep paying Jobber for the software exactly as you do today.
  • We change the processing underneath it by connecting Authorize.net, which is a connection Jobber supports itself. You are not being asked to run a workaround.
  • Your rate stops being one flat number. You pay what each card actually costs, plus a flat 0.6 percent, so a debit card on file no longer costs you the same as a premium rewards card.
  • Before you commit to anything, I confirm the exact feature list in writing. Card on file, automatic payments and the Jobber card reader are the three to pin down, and I will not ask you to sign until you have that in your hand.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee. If it ever stops being worth it, you leave.
What You Get
You stay in Jobber
You told me your entire business runs off Jobber and you are not interested in bolting another app onto it. Agreed. Jobber allows an outside processor through its own Authorize.net connection, so your software stays yours and only the processing underneath changes. You keep paying Jobber for the subscription and keep invoicing the way you do now.
True-cost pricing instead of one flat rate
You pay interchange, the real cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.6 percent. That is the entire markup. Today you pay 2.7 percent whether the client pays with a debit card or a premium rewards card, and the cheap ones are quietly paying for the expensive ones. On true cost each card is priced for what it is, and the debit sales are where you get the most back.
A real person, and no lock-in
You get my direct phone and email, plus in-house support that can escalate an issue and 24/7 phone support behind it. One $25 monthly fee covers the gateway, the payment links, statements, PCI compliance and support, so there is nothing else on the bill. No contract, no cancellation fee, no early termination fee.

No Lock-In, And The Rate Is Not Frozen

The thing that bothers me about your current setup is not really the 2.7 percent. It is that four years went by and the number never moved, because a flat rate is not something you can negotiate. On true-cost pricing the bulk of what you pay is interchange, which is set by Visa and Mastercard and is the same for everybody, and the only part we control is that flat 0.6 percent. That part is a conversation, now and later as you grow. And there is no contract holding you to any of it.

  • No contract, no cancellation fee, no early termination fee. You can leave whenever you want and we will help you close the account out properly.
  • The 0.6 percent is our markup, not a fixed cost. As your volume grows, that is the number that comes down.
  • You keep paying Jobber directly for the software, so nothing about this ties you to us to keep running your business.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.6 percent. That is the whole markup over the real cost of the card. No tiers, no padding, nothing hidden.
  • You stop paying 2.7 percent on debit. A debit card on file costs a fraction of that to run, and on true-cost pricing you keep the difference instead of Jobber keeping it.
  • Jobber stays exactly where it is. Scheduling, invoicing, your website and your bookkeeping do not move. We connect Authorize.net underneath and replace the processing only.
  • One $25 monthly fee and that is the only fixed cost. It covers the gateway, payment links, statements, PCI compliance and support. We keep you PCI compliant as part of the service.
  • The feature list goes to you in writing first. Card on file, automatic payments and the card reader all get confirmed before you sign anything. I would rather lose the deal than surprise you after it.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee. You have my direct line and email, in-house support behind me, and 24/7 phone support behind that.

Here is the math on a $10,000 month. On Jobber's flat 2.7 percent you are paying about $270 in card fees, and that same 2.7 percent is charged on a debit card that costs a fraction of it to run. On interchange plus a flat 0.6 percent the card fees land around $240 on a normal mix of cards, and lower than that if a good share of your clients pay with debit. I am not going to pretend that is a life-changing number at your volume, and I am not going to put a big annual savings figure on this page that I cannot stand behind yet. What I can stand behind is this: your card cost starts following the cards your clients actually use, the markup is the only piece either of us controls and it is on the table, and after four years of a number that never moved, that is the part that is actually worth your time.
