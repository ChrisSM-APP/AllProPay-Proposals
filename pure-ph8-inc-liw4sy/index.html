The Flexibility You Asked For

On the water side you absorb the card fee today and build it into your pricing. You already pass the fee to the customer on the electric side, so you know how it works. If you ever want to do the same here and bring your card cost to zero, the same account switches over. No new application, no new equipment, same workflow.
