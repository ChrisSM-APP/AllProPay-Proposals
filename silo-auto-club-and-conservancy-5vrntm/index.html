Where We Are

Thanks for taking the call, Jeff. You were straight with me that you were not expecting it, and you still walked me through how the club actually runs, so let me do the same for you here. You bill about 30 storage members a month on a card you keep on file, and you invoice events that can land anywhere between a thousand dollars and twenty thousand. Roughly nine out of ten of those charges never see a card reader. That is the single most expensive way there is to take money on Square, and it is the part I can fix. I have priced two ways to fix it and I am telling you plainly which one I would pick.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • The 30 recurring storage accounts keep billing automatically on the card you already have on file. Same day of the month, same amount, nothing for your members to re-enter.
  • Event invoices go out the same way they do now, by email with a link to pay.
  • A single event charge as large as $20,000 has to clear without anyone freezing the money.
  • You told me you absorb the card cost until it gets too big to absorb. This is about making that decision once instead of every invoice.
What You Get

Backed and flexible

Across 30 members that is about $320 a month, or $3,834 a year, on nothing more than a charge that runs itself. On Edge your cost on those card sales is $0, and your whole monthly is a flat $35. Square also bills you that same 3.5% when a member pays with a debit card, which costs a small fraction of what a rewards credit card costs to run. The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked a good question on the call that nobody there had a clean answer to, which was why storage and food and beverage are on two different systems in the first place. Here is my honest answer, plus the two ways to price the side I can actually help with today.

  • Option 1. AllProPay Edge. This is the one I am recommending. Your cost on card sales is $0. The card price and the list price are both shown before anyone pays, on the invoice and at enrollment, and members who pay by check or bank transfer pay list price. Your whole monthly is a flat $35. Estimated $3,400 to $7,700 a year stays in the club.
  • Option 2. The AllProPay Rate. You keep absorbing the cost the way you do today, but instead of one flat retail rate you pay the true wholesale cost set by Visa and Mastercard plus a flat 0.60% and 5 cents a charge, with the same $35 a month. Nothing is ever shown to a member. On your book that is an estimated $250 to $315 a month against about $320 today. It is a genuine improvement on the debit side and it is honestly close to a wash if your members are mostly on rewards credit cards. I would rather tell you that now than have you find it on a statement in March.
  • You are not locked into either one. Start on Edge, and if the board ever decides it would rather absorb the cost, we move you to the AllProPay Rate on the same account with no reapplication and no fee. It works the other way too. You can also apply Edge to event invoices only and keep absorbing the storage billing, which is close to how you already handle it.
  • On running both sides as one account. The system your food and beverage runs on keeps its own processing built in, so there is no way to put both books on one account without changing that point of sale. That is a real conversation and I am happy to price it, but it is a bigger decision than this one and I did not want to bundle it in here.
Why This Is the Right Move
