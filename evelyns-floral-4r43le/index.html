Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.7 percent and 10 cents. That is the whole markup over the true cost of the card. No tiers, no padding.
  • You stop paying Square's flat 2.6 percent on debit. Debit and everyday cards cost a fraction of that at true cost, and on true-cost pricing you keep the difference.
  • An upgraded register, and we move your items over for you. You send the spreadsheet you already have and we rebuild it with pictures. Same handheld tap-to-pay on deliveries and at school events.
  • No long lease, no contract, no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.
  • Zero card cost is there if you ever want it. The same account flips to dual pricing down the road, no rebuild, whenever you and Kelsey are ready.

Here is the math on a $15,000 month. On Square's flat 2.6 percent plus 10 cents you are paying somewhere around $400 a month in card fees, and more of that than you would think is on debit cards that barely cost anything to run. On interchange plus 0.7 percent and 10 cents, with most of your volume taken in person, the processing lands closer to $330 to $360, and the EPOS Now register is about $80 a month. So the card savings cover most of the upgraded system and you come out with a better register and true-cost pricing instead of a flat rate you can never lower. These are estimates from the volume and card mix you shared, not a guarantee. One Square statement makes them exact.
