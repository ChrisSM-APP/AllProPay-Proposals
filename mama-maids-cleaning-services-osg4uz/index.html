How the Two Pricing Models Differ
Stripe uses flat-rate pricing. Brian pays 2.9% + $0.30 on every transaction, regardless of whether the customer used a debit card (which costs Stripe almost nothing) or a high-rewards credit card (which costs Stripe more). Stripe keeps the spread between what they charge and what the card networks actually cost them.

AllProPay uses interchange-plus pricing. The card networks' actual cost is passed through at exact cost, and our markup is a flat, transparent rate on top. When a customer pays with a debit card, Brian pays the regulated debit rate (around 0.05% + $0.21) plus our markup. When a customer pays with a credit card, Brian pays the credit interchange (around 1.65%-2.10%) plus our markup. He gets the savings on debit transactions instead of Stripe pocketing them.

Why this matters for a residential cleaning business: a meaningful share of recurring customer payments come in on personal debit cards rather than credit cards. Stripe charges 2.9% on every debit transaction regardless. AllProPay charges the actual debit interchange (a fraction of 1%) plus our markup. That single mix difference is where most of the savings comes from.
