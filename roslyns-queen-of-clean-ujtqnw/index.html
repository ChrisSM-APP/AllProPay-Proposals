The Issue with the Current Setup

Stripe's flat rate is not the right structure for a $35k/month service business

Stripe charges a flat 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction, which means you are paying the same rate whether a customer puts $50 on their card or $2,000. For a cleaning business processing at this volume, interchange-plus pricing - where you pay the actual card cost plus a fixed margin - is almost always more cost-effective. The difference between 3.3% flat and a properly structured interchange-plus program at your volume is meaningful.

Square is designed for simple retail - not a spa business with large tickets and inventory

Square works well for quick-service businesses, but it is not purpose-built for a spa that invoices, tracks inventory, and processes large ticket sales. At 2.5%+ per transaction, a $1,500 spa ticket costs $37.50 in fees. The POS we would put Gary on handles the same workflows at a lower overall cost - and the cash discount program shifts the processing fee to the customer entirely.

On the Jobber integration: We have confirmed that the Jobber payment integration continues to work normally. Your invoices, scheduling, and client records all stay inside Jobber. Only the processor that powers the payment changes. This was the sticking point with the bank setup that did not work out previously - it is not an issue here.
What We Recommend
Roslyn's Queen of Clean - EDGE Cash Discount Program

We move the cleaning business from Stripe to a compliant EDGE (cash discount) setup. Under this program, customers who pay by card are charged a small service fee that covers the processing cost. Your effective processing cost drops to zero. Jobber stays in place - customers pay through the same flow they use today.

  • Your processing cost: $0 on transactions
  • Customers pay a disclosed service fee (compliant, properly registered)
  • Jobber integration: unchanged
  • Platform fee: $25/month
Prestige Spa - EDGE + Purpose-Built POS

We replace the Square setup with a full POS system built for service businesses - one that handles invoicing, inventory, daily operations, and appointment-based sales. The same EDGE cash discount program applies, so Gary's processing cost on transactions also drops to zero. The POS monthly cost is $70 to $75 per month.

  • Full POS with invoicing, inventory, and daily operations
  • EDGE cash discount: processing cost on transactions drops to $0
