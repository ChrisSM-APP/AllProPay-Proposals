What's Not Working

1. The Lightspeed Acquisition Made Things Worse

ShopKeep was functional until Lightspeed bought it. Since then, the experience has declined and the processing is bundled into their ecosystem — meaning your payment rates are set by the same company that already isn't serving you well. You have no recourse.

2. Events Are a Manual Nightmare

Before every event like Comic Con, you have to manually remove from inventory every item you're bringing, log each sale by hand on the day, then reimport everything that didn't sell when you get back. On a catalog of thousands of items with multiple artists, that's hours of work every time. It shouldn't work this way.

3. Ricochet Locks You In the Same Trap

Ricochet is purpose-built for consignment, which is why it made your shortlist. But it does its own payment processing at roughly 3%, and it does not share that relationship with anyone. If you move to Ricochet, you trade one locked system for another — at roughly $200/month in software fees plus 3% on every dollar you process, with no leverage and no exit.
