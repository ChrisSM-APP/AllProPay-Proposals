Cash discounting, in plain English

Two prices at the point of sale. The customer chooses how to pay.

Your menu shows the card price. Customers paying cash get a discount off that price. Customers paying by card pay the listed price, which covers the processing cost.

The result: your card processing costs are absorbed at the point of sale rather than coming out of your margin. Cash sales become more attractive to customers, and card sales no longer eat into your profit.

We handle the compliant setup based on California rules and how your prices are displayed. You don't have to figure that part out.
