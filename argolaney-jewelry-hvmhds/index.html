What an AllProPay Setup Would Look Like
In-store, terminal-only
A clean countertop terminal that swipes, dips, and taps just like the Clover Mini. ARM USA keeps doing what it does today. Nothing on your back office changes.
WooCommerce, plugged in
An AllProPay gateway connects to your existing WooCommerce checkout. Same shopping cart, same site, same customer experience. The processor behind it is just cheaper.
Interchange-plus, no padding
You pay the card-brand cost plus a small, fixed markup. No tiered rates, no surprise downgrades, no contract, no early-termination fee.

Why This Is a Fair Look
  • You keep everything that works. ARM USA stays. WooCommerce stays. Your checkout flow stays. We swap the processor under the hood, not the way you run the store.
  • No fee pass-through. You said absorbing the fees is the right call for a jewelry customer base. We agree. This setup is built around lower absorbed cost, not surcharging.
  • One bill, one team. In-store and online go through the same AllProPay account. Same support number, same statement, same monthly platform fee.
  • No contract, no exit fee. If the numbers are not better than what you have on Clover, you walk. We only stay in business if you stay because you want to.
  • You set the timeline. You told Kurt this could be a couple of months before you digest it. That is fine. We do not chase, and we do not pressure.
