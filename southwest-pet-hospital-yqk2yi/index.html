The Flexibility You Asked For

You told me you are not the first one to jump on a new idea, and that is fair. So the account is built so you are never stuck with the choice you make on day one.

  • Start on AllProPay Edge. The client who chooses to pay by card covers the card fee, so your cost on card sales is $0. Cash and check clients pay the posted price.
  • If you would rather keep absorbing the fee, we move you to AllProPay Rate on the same account with no reapplication. That is interchange plus a small flat markup, so you see the true network cost and exactly what we make on it.
  • You can also run Edge and change your mind. If a client ever pushes back, you call me and I switch you the same day. That is how the account is set up, not a favour.
