The goal is simple. Keep billing the way you bill now, stop absorbing the fees yourself, and keep everything reconciling in QuickBooks.

Backed and flexible

The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked to keep your billing simple and change as little as possible. The account is built that way.

  • Run it on the $25/month invoicing platform, or step up to the $75/month plan that writes payments straight back into QuickBooks. Your call, same program either way.
  • Phone, invoice, and counter all live on one account, so there is nothing new to learn.
