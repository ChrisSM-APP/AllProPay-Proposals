The Flexibility You Asked For

You are not locked into one way of running this. The account is built so you can change the model whenever it suits the salon, without switching processors or learning new hardware.

  • Start on Edge so card sales cost you nothing, and see how your clients respond.
  • If you ever decide to absorb the fee yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus pricing on the same account, no new paperwork.
  • Run dual pricing on services and keep simple cash pricing on retail, or any mix you want.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • The single biggest cost on Square, the card fee, goes away.
  • Next-day funding, so your money is not sitting for days.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If it is not right, you walk.
  • Direct support and underwriting, not a 1-800 queue.
