The Flexibility You Asked For

I told you I would send two options, so here are both, and here is my honest read on them. I am recommending the first one. The second is a real choice, not filler, and you can move between them later on the same account without reapplying.

  • Option 1, Edge. This is the one on this page and the one I am recommending. Your cost on card sales is $0, your whole monthly is $25, and the card fee moves to the card-paying customer. Cash and check customers pay list price. Estimated $2,900 to $3,400 a year stays in the business.
  • Option 2, interchange-plus. You keep absorbing the card cost the way you do on Square today, but instead of one flat retail rate you pay the true wholesale cost set by Visa and Mastercard plus a flat 0.7% and 5 cents, with the same $25 a month. Straight with you: at about $10,000 a month that lands somewhere between roughly break-even and a few hundred dollars a year better than Square, and almost all of the win comes off cards you tap at the job rather than ones paid off an invoice. It is the right answer if you never want an adjustment shown to a customer. It is not where the money is.
  • You are not locked into either. Start on Edge, and if you ever decide you would rather absorb the cost yourself, we move you to interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication and no new hardware.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your cost on card sales goes to $0 and your whole monthly is a flat $25.

  • You are running two businesses. The same pricing carries to the second one when you are ready for it.
