The Flexibility You Asked For

You are starting on Edge so the card fee comes off your books from day one. You are not locked into it, and the account moves with you if you ever want to change how the fee is handled. For your largest commercial invoices, you also have a near-free ACH lane.

  • Start on Edge now. The card-paying customer covers the fee, so your net cost on card sales is zero.
  • Send the big jobs by ACH. A bank-to-bank payment on a $20k to $40k invoice clears for a flat $5, with no card adjustment for the customer at all.
  • Switch to interchange-plus whenever you want. Same account, same reader. You absorb the fee and we price it at cost plus a small fixed markup, no surprises.
