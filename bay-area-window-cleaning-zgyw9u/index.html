What We Found

Flat rate regardless of card type

Jobber Payments charges 2.9% on every transaction, regardless of whether the customer used a basic Visa, a premium rewards card, or Amex. Interchange - the underlying cost of processing - varies significantly by card type. Visa consumer cards typically settle between 1.5% and 1.8%. You are paying a full point or more above that cost on 63% of your volume.

Per-transaction fees on 48 monthly charges

At $0.30 per credit card transaction, Bay Area Window Cleaning is paying approximately $14.40/month - $173/year - just in per-transaction fees, on top of the percentage. That charge does not exist on a properly structured interchange-plus account.

ACH charged as a percentage

Jobber charges 1% on bank/ACH payments with a $10 cap per transaction. On your 6 ACH transactions totaling $4,408, that came to $45.09 in a single month. Bank transfers should cost a few cents each - not a percentage of the amount. This adds roughly $540/year in unnecessary fees.
