True-cost card pricing
You pay interchange, the real cost set by Visa and Mastercard for each card, plus a flat 0.50 percent and 8 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup. On a bundled rate you pay the same on a debit card as on a rewards card. On true cost you pay what each card actually costs and keep the difference, and at $350,000 a month the difference is real money. One flat $25 a month covers the account, statements, PCI and support. Nothing else.
Real support, no lock-in
A named rep, me, who opens the account, walks the connection with you, and handles any chargeback paperwork alongside you. No contract, no cancellation fee, month to month. A store doing your volume deserves a person who picks up, not a ticket queue.

Built Around How Titan Games Sells

Your customers pay at the counter, and a few pay online. The account is set up around that, and around one thing you were clear on: the fee stays with the business, never on the customer. There is no surcharge, no cash price versus card price, and nothing changes on the receipt.

  • At the counter. Tap, dip, or swipe. Interchange plus 0.50 percent and 8 cents, whichever card comes out of the wallet.
  • Online orders. Same account, same markup. Online interchange runs a little higher than in-store, and that is the networks' pricing, not ours.
  • Your timing. You have about a year left on your current agreement. Get the statement to me now and you will know exactly what the switch is worth before you have to decide anything.
  • No contract either way. Month to month. If we ever stop earning it, you walk, and there is no fee for walking.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.50 percent and 8 cents. That is the whole markup over the true cost of each card. No tiers, no padding, no bundle.
  • At your volume, tenths of a point are real money. Every 0.10 percent off your all-in rate is about $350 a month, $4,200 a year, at $350,000 a month in card sales.
  • A bundled rate overcharges you on the easy cards. Debit and everyday cards cost a fraction of what a rewards card costs. On a bundle you pay the same on both. On true cost you pay what each one actually is.
  • Your customers never see a fee. You told me that matters and it is built in. Same prices, same receipt, same experience at the counter.
  • No contract, no cancellation fee, one $25 monthly fee. You are coming off a contract. You should not have to sign another one to find out if we are better.

Here is what the pricing looks like on a real sale. Say a customer buys $60 of cards and a sleeve pack. Across a month of about $350,000 in card sales, the card side lands somewhere around $7,400 to $9,100 in total, plus the $25 platform fee. These are estimates on the volume you gave me, not a guarantee. Your card mix and your real monthly total set the exact number, and one statement gives me both.
