Executive Summary

We reviewed The Shack on Broadway's December 2025 processing statement from Toast. The restaurant processed $118,137 in card volume that month and paid $3,647 in processing fees - a 3.08% effective rate. For a restaurant doing consistent volume at this level, 3.08% is above the benchmark range we typically see on a well-structured account. The core issue goes deeper than a single fee: Toast packages the POS and the payment processing as one product. The processing rate and the POS contract are inseparable - which means the only path to a lower rate is replacing the POS with a system that lets you choose your own processor.

After a line-by-line review, here is what the numbers show:

  • An effective rate of 3.08% - above the 3.00% threshold where we classify processing costs as very high for a restaurant at this volume.
  • A locked pricing model: because Toast bundles POS and processing, you cannot switch processors without switching the POS.
  • A keyed transaction rate of 3.50% applied to $15,134 in monthly volume - the highest-cost segment on the statement.

There are two paths to fixing this. The first keeps Toast in place and runs AllProPay processing alongside it - least disruption, fastest savings. The second replaces the POS entirely with a system like EPOSnow, paired with AllProPay processing. Our estimate is $6,000 - $9,600 in annual savings under either path, based on your actual statement numbers.

What We Found

1. Toast bundles the POS and processing - the rate is tied to the POS contract

Toast is not just a payment processor - it is an integrated POS and processor sold as one product. The 2.49% card-present rate and 3.50% keyed rate are built into the package. You cannot call Toast and negotiate the processing rate independently; it is part of the POS agreement.

There are two ways to address this. One is to run AllProPay processing alongside Toast - keeping the POS exactly as it is and routing card settlements through AllProPay separately. The other is a full POS replacement with a system like EPOSnow, which pairs with AllProPay processing and covers the same restaurant functionality. Which path makes sense depends on your operation and how much change you want to take on.

2. 3.08% effective rate - above what this volume should cost

At $118,137 in monthly volume, a restaurant on a transparent interchange-plus structure typically runs an effective rate between 2.25% and 2.55%. At 3.08%, you are paying an estimated $640 to $980 per month more than you would on a comparable interchange-plus setup. Over a full year, that is $7,680 to $11,760 in fees above what this account should cost.
