What You Get
Your bank stays your bank
M&T keeps your loans, your discounted banking fees, and your retirement accounts. Switching who processes your cards does not move a dollar of that and does not put your banking relationship on the table. Your funding still settles into the same M&T account the next morning. The only thing that changes is the company handling the cards.
True-cost pricing, no tiers
You pay interchange, the true cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.6 percent and 5 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup. What it removes is the tier game: the qualified, mid-qualified, and non-qualified buckets that quietly reprice your biggest sales into the most expensive category.
Real support, and no contract
A named rep who answers the phone and handles chargeback paperwork with you. We confirm in writing how card payments hand off to RRO Writer before anything changes, and if it cannot be done cleanly we will tell you and you stay where you are. No contract and no cancellation fee.
