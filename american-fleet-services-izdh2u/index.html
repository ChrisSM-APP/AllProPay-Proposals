Why Not a Flat-Rate Box

You may have looked at the simple all-in-one options like Square or Clover. They are easy to start, but for a shop running about $100k a month in invoices they quietly cost more and give you less control. Here is the honest comparison.

 A flat-rate box (Square, Clover)AllProPay Edge
Who absorbs the card feeThe business does, on every card saleThe card-paying customer — your cost is $0
Debit and lower-cost cardsCharged the same flat rate as credit, so you overpayYou are not locked into one high rate on every card type
Invoice and keyed paymentsLand in the highest flat tier, often around 3.3% or moreHandled at $0 cost to you under dual pricing
Your equipmentBuy or lease a terminal, and Clover hardware is usually tied to the processor that sold itUse ShopMonkey plus Tap to Pay — nothing to buy, no lock-in
Getting your moneyAggregator accounts are known to hold or freeze funds on large or unusual batchesYour own direct merchant account with next-day funding
Leaving laterTied to the device and the processor that sold itNo contract and no cancellation fee — if we ever stop earning it, you walk

Comparison reflects how flat-rate aggregator platforms are generally structured. Your exact experience depends on the plan and reseller.
