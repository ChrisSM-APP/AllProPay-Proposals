Where We Are

Raul, price was never your problem. You already pass the card fee to the customer, most of your thirty cars a month are paid in cash, and you told Matt straight out what actually keeps you up: three years ago two disputed car sales at roughly $6,000 to $7,000 each cost you the sales, and then cost you your processing. Two or three processors dropped the account. That is what this proposal is built to answer.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • An account that holds. Opened in the business name, underwritten up front, with the file reviewed before anything goes live rather than sixty days after your first deposit.
  • Every card sale backed by a signed card authorization page, so a disputed car has proof behind it instead of your word against the buyer's.
  • Chargeback protection and dispute support on the account, so you find out the day a dispute is filed, not weeks later.
  • The card fee keeps moving to the card-paying customer, exactly the way you already run it.
  • Straight answers on the credit check and the underwriting file before you sign anything.
  • A flat $25 a month, free countertop terminal, no contract, next-day funding.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash and check customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Every card sale defended in writing
A card authorization page comes with the account. The buyer signs at the desk, the signed authorization is stored with the transaction, and if that sale is ever disputed you have a document to produce. You also get chargeback alerts and help building the response. Nobody can promise a dispute outcome, but a $6,000 car with a signed authorization on file is a very different fight than a $6,000 car with nothing.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked what happens with the credit check and the old processing history. Here is the honest version, so there are no surprises.

  • The SSN and the credit check are standard on every merchant application in the United States, including the ones you already signed. Underwriting uses them to confirm who owns the business. A score around 620 is not by itself a decline, and the business, the volume, and the deposit history carry more weight than the score does.
  • If anything comes back on the underwriting check, including a MATCH listing from the processors that dropped you in 2023, Matt tells you what it says and what it means before you sign anything. You will not find out after the first deposit.
  • The account opens in the business name instead of Raul's personal name. Being written as a sole proprietor is part of why you have been priced and treated as high risk.
  • Start on Edge with the fee on the card-paying customer, exactly as you run today. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication.
