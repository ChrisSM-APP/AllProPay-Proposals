Where We Are

You walked me through exactly how you get paid, so let me give it back to you the way I heard it. Small jobs get collected on the spot, tapped on the phone. Anything over about $1,500 gets an invoice with a deposit on it, usually 50 percent, and you set the percentage yourself depending on the job. The link stays live until it is paid, because you go by when the work is finished, not by whatever date the system made you type in. Big jobs come in as checks and you mark them paid. A few customers push the money from their own bank, so that costs you nothing and it should stay exactly that way. Nothing about that has to change. This is that same day, run on our side instead of Square's.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Invoices with a deposit. Set the deposit percentage per job. Save the ones you use most, so a job over $1,500 is one click at 50 percent and you can still drop it to 30 for a customer you want to look after.
  • The link stays live until it is paid. Same as now. Put a date on it or do not, the customer can still pay when the job is actually done.
  • Card on file, so you stop chasing the balance. They pay the deposit, you keep the card in a protected vault, and when the job is finished the balance goes on that card. No second link, no reminder chain.
  • You hear about a decline. Right now Square tells your customer the card failed and you find out later. Here the alert comes to you the moment it happens.
  • Tap on the phone in the field. Same as your setup today, on the phone that is already in his pocket.
  • Checks and bank transfers stay as they are. Mark them paid, no fee, nothing routed through us that does not need to be.
