Here is the part most people miss. You add 3.7% at the invoice, and Square charges its own rate on the whole amount, including the 3.7% you just added. Depending on how the customer pays, Square takes somewhere between 2.6% and 3.5%, so on your invoice work the adjustment you collect is barely covering what they take. At about $7,500 a month you are collecting roughly $278 from customers and handing most of it right back.

On Edge it is not a guess. Your cost on card sales is $0, on every lane, every time, and the full ticket lands in your account the next business day. Nothing changes for your customers either. They are already paying an adjustment today.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You have a loan sitting on the Square account, so we are not asking you to pull the plug on anything. The account is built to run alongside what you have.

  • Keep Square open and running whatever share you want to keep the loan satisfied, and route the rest of the work through us. You are not choosing one or the other on day one.
  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication.
  • Turn the bank-transfer lane on for the biggest builds, where a $10,000 job clears for $5 instead of card rates. Use it when it makes sense, ignore it when it does not.
