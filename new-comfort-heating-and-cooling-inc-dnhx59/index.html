The Flexibility You Asked For

Today you absorb the card fee and price it into your work, and this proposal keeps it that way. If you ever decide you would rather pass the fee to card-paying customers instead, the same account switches to that program. No new application, no new equipment, same workflow.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today.
  • Switch to dual pricing whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run a few months on IC+, see the actual cost on your statement, and decide if you want to move it to the customer. No penalty either way.
