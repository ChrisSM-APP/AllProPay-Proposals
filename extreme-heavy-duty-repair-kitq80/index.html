The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked to keep this simple and not get boxed in. The account is built to flex with you.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead of posting it to customers, I move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account, no new application.
  • Every payment keeps syncing to QuickBooks either way, so the accounting side never changes.
  • Want to take a card in person at the counter down the road? We add a terminal or Tap to Pay to the same account whenever you are ready. You are not locked into links only.
