What We Investigated on Your Behalf

Before presenting this plan, we worked through every available path to make this transition as seamless as possible for you and your clients.

Path 1 - Authorize.net Integration with Jobber

Authorize.net previously worked with Jobber and was our original plan. Jobber has since deliberately degraded this integration to push merchants toward their own payment product (Jobber Payments). Features including recurring billing and tip collection no longer function reliably through Authorize.net. This path was ruled out early.

Path 2 - Token Export from Jobber (March 13, 2026)

We contacted Jobber support directly requesting an export of stored client card tokens in JSON format for transfer to a new gateway. Jobber's response: "Jobber does not offer an option to migrate saved card holder data to an external platform." We pushed back noting the underlying vault runs through Stripe, but Jobber confirmed they will not facilitate the transfer regardless.

Path 3 - Stripe Network Token Import at FluidPay (March 6-9, 2026)

We contacted FluidPay directly asking whether Stripe network tokens could be imported. FluidPay's response: "We do not handle network tokens." FluidPay does support vault imports from certain gateways, but Stripe's CIM tokens are not in a transferable format. The card data is locked by design.

Conclusion - The Lock-In Is on Stripe's End, Not Ours

Stripe intentionally prevents card data from leaving their platform. This protects their recurring billing revenue and is a known industry practice. AllProPay investigated every available path. The only viable option is a one-time card re-save from existing clients through Jobber - which is simpler to execute than it sounds, and only needs to happen once.

Here is exactly how this works. There is no forced cutover and no disruption to how your team operates day-to-day.

3
New clients are captured in FluidPay from day one.
Any client who pays through Jobber after the switch automatically saves their card in FluidPay. No action needed from you or them.
