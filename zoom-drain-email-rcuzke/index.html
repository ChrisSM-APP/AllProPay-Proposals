Where We Are

Your office passed along the short version when we called: you run payments through QuickBooks, and most of what you collect comes in online against an invoice rather than at a counter. That is the exact setup where card fees do the most quiet damage, because every invoice a customer pays by card gives roughly three and a half percent of that job to the processor before you see a cent of it. What I have put together keeps QuickBooks and your invoicing exactly as they are, and takes that cost off your books.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • QuickBooks stays. Your invoices, your customer list, and your books do not move anywhere.
  • Customers keep paying the way they already do, online against an invoice, by phone, or in the field.
  • Your cost on card sales goes to $0 instead of coming off the top of every job.
  • One flat monthly cost, no contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
The price on the invoice is the card price. A customer who pays by cash or check pays the lower price instead, and a customer who pays by card pays the posted price, which carries a 3.85% adjustment disclosed before they pay. Either way, your cost on that card sale is $0.
Your QuickBooks workflow does not change
We connect to the QuickBooks you already use, so invoices go out the same way, payments post against them the same way, and your books reconcile the same way. We have done this integration with thousands of businesses. Nothing about how your office works on a Monday morning changes.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You have not had a real conversation with us yet, so nothing here locks you into one way of working. The account is built to move.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you would rather absorb the fee yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus pricing on the same account with no reapplication.
  • Run it on every invoice or only on the jobs where it makes sense. That is your call, job by job.
  • Take payment however the customer wants to pay: a link in the invoice, a card read over the phone, or a tap in the field.
