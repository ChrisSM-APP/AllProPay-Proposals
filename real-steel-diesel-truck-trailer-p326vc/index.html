Where We Are

You have already done the harder half of this. Invoices that run through Converge carry the card fee, your customers pay it, and business carries on. The counter is the piece that never got finished: the terminal absorbs its own fees, because a customer standing in front of you has no time to let a new fee sink in. Add a real concern about chargebacks, and about $50,000 a month in card volume, and it is worth getting this right once.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Keep passing the card fee where you already do. No going backward.
  • Stop absorbing fees at the terminal, without ambushing counter customers at the last second.
  • A straight answer on chargebacks: what one costs and who helps when it happens.
  • One account and one statement, instead of a Converge side and a terminal side that behave differently.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash, check, and ACH customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Same counter, same workflow
Nothing changes about how you ring a sale. The difference is that the card price is posted before anyone reaches the counter - on the wall, on the ticket, on the invoice - so it is old news by the time a customer pays. That is the piece your current setup is missing: disclosure up front instead of a fee at the finish line.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked about the options, so here they are - all three run on the same account, and you can pick and choose, rail by rail, whether the fee is passed on or absorbed. The system does the math automatically either way.

  • AllProPay Edge (my recommendation): one posted price on every card, your cost on card sales is $0, and the whole account runs on $25 a month.
  • AllProPay Rate: if you would rather absorb the fee, you pay interchange - the true card cost set by Visa and Mastercard - plus one flat markup. On your July numbers that lands at an estimated $1,100 to $1,250 a month versus the $1,311 you were charged, with no month-end extras and every fee visible. And you can still pass fees on where you want to - automatically, instead of managing it by hand.
  • Mix the two: what you do today, done properly. Keep passing the fee where it works and absorb it where you choose - except the system handles every rail, including the counter, and the math is automatic.
  • An ACH lane for the big invoices: 0.50% capped at $10, so a $10,000 job clears for $10.
  • Since we talked about Edge Plus: once the posted rate is running on every rail and the shop is holding $10,000+ a month, we can step you up to the version of Edge that pays the shop a monthly rebate. I will bring the exact numbers when we get there.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Card sales stop costing the shop money on every rail - counter, keyed, debit, and AMEX included.
  • Debit is the quiet one: a surcharge program can never touch debit cards, so on your current setup that cost is yours forever. Edge is dual pricing, so the posted price applies to debit too.
  • $25 a month all-in replaces about $42 a month in gateway and monthly-discount extras on your July statement.
  • Chargebacks, answered: $15 only when one actually happens - July had zero - and I help you respond, with a real person on the phone.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.

Your average ticket is about $1,500. Today, when that job is swiped at the counter, roughly $39 of it goes to the processor before you see it. On Edge that same swipe costs the shop $0, and across the year the rails you absorb today add up to an estimated $5,500 to $7,000 staying in the business.
