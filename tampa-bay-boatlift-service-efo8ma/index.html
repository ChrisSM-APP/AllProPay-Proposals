The Amex Question

You said roughly 90% of your customers pay with Amex. That is the single most important fact for choosing the right program, and it is why a generic interchange-plus pitch would have wasted your time.

With the POS+ surcharge program, Amex is treated the same as Visa and Mastercard. The 3% compliant credit-card surcharge applies across all three brands. You do not lose money on the Amex transactions because the surcharge offsets the processing cost on every credit card, not just the cheap ones.

Why this matters for your business: Most processors quote a low headline rate that only shows up on basic Visa transactions, then quietly charge full retail on Amex. With 90% Amex volume, that pricing model would crush you. The surcharge program sidesteps the whole problem because the cardholder pays the surcharge no matter which brand they hand you.
