The Two Ways We Can Set It Up

You absorb the fee today, so you have a clean choice. Neither one changes how a customer pays you at the counter.

  • Lower transparent rate. Keep absorbing the fee like you do now, just at a real interchange-plus cost with your debit priced as debit. Nothing changes for the customer. You simply pay less.
  • Dual pricing. Post a cash price and a card price. Card-paying customers cover the processing, cash and debit customers pay less, and your absorbed cost drops to roughly the $25 platform fee. Common in retail, fully compliant when set up right.
  • Start one, switch later. Same account either way. Run the lower rate, watch a real statement, and move to dual pricing if and when it makes sense. No penalty.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your 2.70 percent is not crazy on the surface, but you are paying a full credit-card rate on roughly $8,900 a month of debit. That is the clearest dollar you are leaving on the table.
  • Interchange-plus prices each card for what it actually is, so a debit-heavy shop like yours benefits the most.
  • Your keyed phone and online orders at 3.50 percent get a proper card-not-present rate instead of a flat tier.
  • If you would rather not pay it at all, dual pricing is a clean, compliant way to move the cost to the customer who chose the card.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee, so the only thing you are committing to is a look at the numbers.
