Same counter, a real person
Keep taking cards on a terminal the way you do now. We supply the equipment if you need it, at no charge. And you get one dedicated rep who owns your account, not a 1-800 queue.

The Two Ways We Can Set It Up

You are absorbing the fee today, so you have a clean choice. Neither one changes how a customer pays you at the counter.

  • Lower transparent rate. Keep absorbing the fee like you do now, just at a real interchange-plus cost instead of a packaged retail markup. Nothing changes for the customer. You simply pay less.
  • Dual pricing. Post a cash price and a card price. Card-paying customers cover the processing, cash and debit customers pay less, and your absorbed cost drops close to zero. Common in retail, fully compliant when set up right.
  • Start one, switch later. Same account either way. Run the lower rate, see a real statement, and move to dual pricing if and when it makes sense. No penalty.
Why This Is Worth a Look
  • Every month you absorb the fee on Chase's packaged pricing, you are paying a markup you cannot see and did not negotiate.
  • Interchange-plus shows you the true cost of each card plus one fixed number. That transparency alone usually saves a retail shop money.
  • If you would rather not pay it at all, dual pricing is a clean, compliant way to hand the card cost to the customer who chose the card.
  • No new system. Keep your counter workflow, keep next-day funding, and get a dedicated rep instead of a call center.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee, so the only thing you are committing to is a look at the numbers.
