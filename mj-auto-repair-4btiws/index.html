What We Found

1. Card-not-present rates near 3.7% on half your volume

Roughly 50% of your monthly volume comes in through Tekmetric's text and email payment links. Those are card-not-present (CNP) transactions, and Tekmetric Payments is charging you 3.5% to 3.7% on them.

That is at the very top of the market for a clean, low-risk auto shop. On $20,000/month of online volume, you are paying roughly $700 to $740 in CNP fees alone, and that is before you add in the in-person side.

2. Bundled rate hides where the markup sits

Integrated payments platforms like Tekmetric Payments (formerly 360 Payments) post one number that lumps everything together. The merchant sees a clean rate, and the actual cost is harder to pull apart.

When we move you to a true interchange-plus structure, the wholesale cost of every transaction is shown directly, with a fixed, transparent markup on top. The savings appear because the bundle stops hiding 0.4% to 0.8% of avoidable overhead, not because we are running anything risky.

3. You absorb every penny of the fees yourself

You made it clear on the call that you do not pass fees to your customers, and you have no intention of starting. We respect that, and this proposal does not change it.

The flip side: every dollar we shave off your processing bill goes directly to your bottom line. There is no offset, no customer-paid component, no shift to anyone else. The estimated savings on this proposal are net profit recovered.

You told us Tekmetric runs your entire shop. The digital estimates, the Affirm, Sunbit, and Klarna financing on jobs over $250, the workflow your team relies on every day. We are not going to ask you to give any of that up.
