What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash and check customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Two lanes, one simple rule
If the customer books and pays online, it stays on Fieldd and Stripe. If you are collecting the card yourself, it goes through your AllProPay portal. That is the whole rule. The portal gives you a virtual terminal for the cards you take over the phone, payment links and invoices for the customer who cannot sign off on site, and recurring billing for the monthly subscriptions you are rolling out. You still mark the job paid in Fieldd, the same way you do now.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.
