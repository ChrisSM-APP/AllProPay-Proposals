What This Is Costing You

Fragmented reporting

With Adyen and Clover running separately, there is no single dashboard showing what your hotel and restaurant brought in together. End-of-month reconciliation means manually pulling from two platforms and combining the numbers yourself.

Two processors, two billing relationships

You are managing two separate processing agreements, two fee structures, and two support contacts. When something goes wrong - a chargeback, a payout question, a terminal issue - you are navigating two different companies.

No path to integration

Your current setup was not built to grow together. As your hotel and restaurant operations become more connected - shared guests, combined loyalty, cross-location staff - a fragmented payment stack becomes a bigger operational drag.
