What We Found

Merchant absorbing all card processing fees

Under the current Bank of America setup, Evergreen pays the full processing cost on every card transaction. There is no mechanism to pass that cost to the cardholder. At 3% on $5,000 a month, that is $150 going to the processor every month whether the business has a strong month or a slow one.

No cash discount or dual pricing program in place

Most standard bank and payment platform accounts do not include dual pricing by default. Merchants have to specifically set it up through a processor that supports it. AllProPay's Edge program is purpose-built for this. Once activated, card customers see and pay the card price (which includes the service fee), and the merchant's effective processing cost drops to zero.

Year-round volume - no seasonal offset

Because Evergreen operates year-round with consistent monthly volume, there is no slow season to absorb the cost. The $1,800 in annual fees is predictable and recurring. That also means savings from switching go into effect immediately across all twelve months.

Is This Legal in Oregon?

This is a fair and important question. Here is the short answer: yes, cash discounting programs are permitted under federal law and in Oregon, when set up correctly.

How it works legally: Under the Dodd-Frank Act, merchants have the right to offer discounts to customers who pay with cash, check, or debit. Visa and Mastercard both have official dual pricing programs that govern how this is disclosed and displayed. AllProPay sets up the program in compliance with those card-brand rules - meaning pricing is clearly shown at the point of sale, on receipts, and at checkout. No surprise charges, no hidden fees for the customer.

Cash discounting and surcharging are different programs with different rules. Surcharging adds a fee on top of the posted price. Cash discounting sets the card price as the standard and offers a lower price for cash. AllProPay uses the dual pricing / cash discount structure, which is cleaner from a compliance standpoint and has no state-level prohibitions in Oregon.

We make sure the program is set up properly based on your location, card-brand rules, and how prices are displayed to customers. AllProPay handles the compliance configuration - you do not have to figure this out yourself.
