What flat-rate pricing actually means at this volume: Square's 1.5% is a negotiated rate - better than their standard 2.6%, and it looks simple. But flat-rate pricing charges the same percentage on every card type. A debit card transaction typically costs a fraction of that to process. A standard Visa credit card costs less than a premium rewards card. At $485,000 a year, the difference between flat-rate and volume-tiered interchange pricing adds up. How much depends on your card mix - which we'd confirm before any decision is made.
