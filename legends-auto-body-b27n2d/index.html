Taking cards the usual way means handing a processor roughly 3% of every card sale, plus monthly fees, plus the equipment charges that soured you the first time. At $5,000 to $15,000 a month in card sales, that is an estimated $150 to $450 a month out of the shop's pocket. On Edge that cost never lands on you: card-paying customers cover their own card cost as a clearly posted adjustment, cash and check customers pay list price, and your cost on card sales is $0, with no monthly fee on this account. The Flexibility You Asked For

This account is built to flex with how the shop actually gets paid, not lock you into one way of taking money.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the shop. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus pricing on the same account, no reapplication.
  • Cash and checks keep working exactly as they do now. Cards are an addition, not a replacement.
  • If insurance work is part of the mix, we set the account up around how those payments actually arrive. That is a two-minute conversation on our call.
