Where We Are

You were clear about how Bosch Marine runs and what you want kept intact. Every job gets invoiced, the invoice goes out with a payment link, the customer picks how they want to pay, and the money lands in your account. Nobody at your shop touches card information, and you want it to stay that way. The one thing you want changed is who carries the card fee: applied automatically at the point of sale, not something you eat off your margin or reconcile by hand at the end of the day.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Everything stays invoice and payment link. Your team never handles card numbers.
  • The card fee is applied automatically on the link at the point of sale, with nothing for you to reconcile later.
  • Your QuickBooks Online workflow and reconciliation stay exactly as they are today.
  • Next-day funding, month-to-month, no exit fees.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash, check, and ACH customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Built for how you invoice
Your AllProPay account sends invoices and payment links the same way you do today, with dual pricing built into the link itself. A card-paying customer sees the clearly disclosed card price and the fee is handled automatically. A customer who pays by bank transfer, check, or cash pays your listed price. Every payment syncs back to QuickBooks Online so your books reconcile the way they always have.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked me to put it in writing so you and your accountant can look at it together. Here is the honest flexibility built into the account.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee yourself, we move you to transparent interchange-plus pricing on the same account, no reapplication.
  • Bank payments stay a first-class lane. Customers who pay by ACH keep paying your listed price on the very same link, so the mix you have today keeps working.
  • Nothing is locked in. Month-to-month, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If we stop earning it, you walk.
