Before You Talk to Your Current Provider

You said you would run this past the person who set up your Electronic Payments account, and you should. Three questions will tell you in five minutes whether what they offer is the same thing as what is on this page.

  • What is the real cost of my cards, and what do you keep above it? Interchange is public. If they cannot show you the split between interchange and their markup, the rate is blended and you cannot check it. Here the markup is one line: 0.40% and 10 cents.
  • Are my business and commercial cards being priced at their real cost? A lot of what comes over your counter is a company card. On a blended rate they cost you the same as everything else. On this account they are priced at what they actually cost.
  • Can the card payments post into QuickBooks Desktop on their own? If the answer is no, a rate match still leaves someone typing every payment in by hand. That is the part of this that saves time every single week, not just money.
