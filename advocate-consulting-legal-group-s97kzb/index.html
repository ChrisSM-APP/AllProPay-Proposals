One Option, Entirely Optional

One thing worth knowing about, and it is optional. On invoices the size yours can be, a bank transfer costs 0.60 percent with a hard cap at $5 per transaction. A card fee scales with the invoice. A bank transfer stops at $5. Some clients will happily pay that way and some will not, and nobody has to change how they bill to find out. To be explicit about what we are not proposing: we are not suggesting you add a card fee to your clients' invoices or change what you charge them. That is not a fit for a firm like yours and we are not going to raise it again.

  • Card stays exactly as it is. Clients who pay by card keep paying by card, the same way, with nothing added to them.
  • Bank transfer is simply available. 0.60 percent, capped at $5 per transaction, which only matters on your largest invoices.
  • Nothing about how you bill has to change. If no client ever uses it, nothing is lost.
