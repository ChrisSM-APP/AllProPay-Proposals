Where We Are

You told me the shop is running about $60,000 a month in card sales and your provider is pulling roughly $1,100 a month back out of your account, and you absorb every dollar of it. About half of that work is quoted jobs paid over the phone, the other half is people tapping at the counter. You have never passed a card fee on to a customer and you are only thinking about it now, so I have written this so you can read the actual number before you decide anything.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Your bank relationship and your next-day funding do not change. Only the back-end processing routes through us.
  • A simple terminal, the same as what you use now. Nothing fancy, nothing to learn.
  • You choose per sale. Leave the posted card price on for everyone, or extend the cash price to a customer you have worked with for years.
  • Phone orders without writing card numbers down on paper.
  • Month to month. No contract, so you can plug it in next to your old machine and decide from there.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program your posted price is the card price. Cash and check customers pay 4% less than what is posted, and that discount is shown clearly before anyone pays. On a card sale your business keeps effectively all of it, so the roughly $1,100 a month you absorb today goes to zero.
Built for phone orders
About half your card work is quoted jobs paid over the phone. Instead of writing a card number down and keying it in later, you text or email the customer a payment link from your computer or your cell. They open their own payment page, see the price and the cash discount, and pay. The pricing is calculated for them, and no card number ever touches your desk.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You said you are not a spur-of-the-moment decision maker and you have never passed a fee on before. So nothing here locks you in, and nothing here is one-way.

  • If your customers push back and you decide you would rather absorb the cost after all, I move you to flat interchange-plus pricing on the same account. No new application, no new paperwork.
  • The cash discount is a switch, not a setting you are stuck with. On for everyone, off for the one customer you would rather not charge, your call sale by sale.
  • You do no invoicing today. The virtual terminal and the online payment page come with the account anyway, so they are there the day you want them and cost nothing until then.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • The roughly $1,100 a month you absorb now goes to $0. That is an estimated $13,200 a year back in the business.
  • No monthly fee at your volume. The terminal, the virtual terminal, and the online payment page are all included.
  • Same bank, same next-day funding, same simple terminal at the counter. Nothing about how you get paid changes.
  • Quoted phone jobs stop meaning card numbers on a notepad. You send a link and they pay themselves.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. Plug it in beside your old machine and if it does not sit right with your customers, unplug it.
