The Flexibility You Asked For

You are starting on Edge so the card fee comes off your books from day one. You are not locked into it, and the account moves with you if you ever want to change how the fee is handled.

  • Start on Edge now. The card-paying customer covers the fee, so your net cost on card sales is zero.
  • Switch to interchange-plus whenever you want. Same account, same terminal. You absorb the fee and we price it transparently at cost plus a small fixed markup.
  • Try one, then the other. Run Edge, watch how your customers respond, and decide. No penalty either way.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • The fee you have been eating on Nuvei every month moves to the card-paying customer, fully disclosed and compliant.
