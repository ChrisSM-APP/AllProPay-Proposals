How It Works

4 steps. Your biller handles it all.

1

Secure the Cards on File

Each patient's card is saved once in the Fluidpay Gateway Vault and assigned a tokenized Customer ID. No card data lives inside SimplePractice or your clinical files.

2

Export the Balance Report

At the end of each billing cycle (daily or weekly), your biller pulls a list of outstanding copays and deductibles from SimplePractice. One quick export - takes under a minute.

3

Upload and Process in Bulk

Your biller drops those balances into a standardized Fluidpay CSV next to each patient's Customer ID. One click fires all transactions simultaneously. No manual card entry. No logging errors.

4

Ledger Stays Clean

$500 processed = $500 deposits in your bank account (gross, daily). Fees are swept in a single invoice at month-end. Your biller bulk-marks SimplePractice entries as "Paid via External Card" - insurance reconciliation stays clean.
