Where We Are

You run Variety Transport lean: you invoice freight, you take payment by phone, and you keep the books in QuickBooks. The frustration you described was simple. QuickBooks takes a cut of every card payment, and on the big card-not-present invoices that cut is real money. As you bring the business back up, the goal is to take cards with zero processing cost to the company, keep your QuickBooks reconciliation exactly as clean as it is now, and route your largest invoices even cheaper.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Card processing cost to the business drops to $0. The fee moves to the card-paying customer.
  • QuickBooks stays your system of record. Payments reconcile back cleanly, with no double entry.
  • You keep taking payments by phone and by emailed link, the way you run today. No storefront, no countertop terminal.
  • Your largest invoices get an ACH lane, so a five-figure ticket clears for a few dollars instead of card rates.
  • No contract and no setup fee, built before you ramp back up so it costs nothing to turn on.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash, check, and ACH customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Built for invoices, not a storefront
You do not need a countertop terminal. Send a secure payment link from your phone or email, or key a card into a virtual terminal, the same way you take payment today. Every card and ACH payment reconciles back to QuickBooks, so your books stay exactly as clean as they are now, without you paying about 2.9% to keep them that way.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.
