Where We Are

Kyle, good talking with you today. Here is what you told me, so you know I was listening. Apex runs on OrbisX: your clients, scheduling, the itemized invoices with your preset services, reminders, and your guys' time clock all live there, and Stripe processes the cards underneath it. So the plan is simple: keep OrbisX for everything it does for you, and move only the payment to us. The card fee stops coming out of your side of the sale.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Edge: cash, check, and bank customers pay the price on the ticket. Card customers see the card price. Your cost on card sales goes to $0.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You told me you like to sit down and read through everything before you decide, and that the process being easy matters as much as the fee. The account is built around that, and it flexes if your situation changes.

  • Start with OrbisX plus the payment link and the app on your phone. When you are ready, we export your preset services from OrbisX and load them into our invoicing. Not a lot of manual work on your side; we take what you have and replicate it.
  • If you ever want to absorb the card fee yourself instead of passing it on, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account, no new application.
  • When you have more guys taking payments, we set them up as users on the account. Our customer service team handles that kind of change for you.
  • No contract, no commitment. If it ever stops working for you, you walk.
