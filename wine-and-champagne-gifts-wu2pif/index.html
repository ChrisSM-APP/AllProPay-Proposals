Chargebacks, since you raised them

Your business has a dispute pattern most processors never think about: the person paying is almost never the person receiving. A husband orders a basket for a client, the charge hits a card statement two weeks later under a name the cardholder half-remembers, and the bank files it as fraud. Add a December where a third of your year lands in one month, and January becomes dispute season. With a big flat-rate processor you get an automated portal and a deadline. With us you get a setup built to stop disputes before they file and a named person on the phone the same day one does. The evidence side is stacked in your favor and most wine merchants never use it: alcohol already ships with adult signature required, which means every delivery generates the exact proof that defeats an item-not-received claim.

  • Descriptor done right. Your card statement line reads as Wine and Champagne Gifts, not an LLC name nobody recognizes. Unrecognized descriptors are one of the biggest sources of avoidable disputes for gifting businesses.
  • Front-line screens on both sites. AVS and CVV on every order, 3-D Secure available on larger tickets, which can shift stolen-card liability to the issuing bank instead of you.
  • Evidence that wins. Tracking plus the adult signature your shipments already require is proof of delivery with a name attached. We help you package it so it is ready before a dispute ever shows up.
  • Same-day response, with help. Dispute windows are short and the portal-only processors let them run out. When one flags, you have a person to call and we respond with the documents the same day.
