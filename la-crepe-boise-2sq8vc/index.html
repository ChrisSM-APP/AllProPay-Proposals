Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.60 percent and 5 cents. That is the whole markup over the true cost of the card. No tiers, no padding.
  • You stop paying Square's flat rate on debit. Square bills a debit card like a rewards credit card. At true cost it is a fraction of that, and you keep the difference.
  • A register with the kitchen display built in, and we move your menu for you. Orders go straight to the line. No third-party app, no re-entering fillings and toppings.
  • No long lease, no contract, no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.

Here is the math on a $15,000 month. On Square's 2.6 percent plus 15 cents, crepe-sized tickets run you about $510 a month in card fees, and more of that than you would think is on debit cards that barely cost anything to run. On interchange plus 0.60 percent and 5 cents, with your sales taken in person, the processing lands closer to $400 to $450, and the register with the kitchen display is about $80 a month all in. So the card savings cover most of the upgraded system, and you come out with a register that works and true-cost pricing instead of a flat rate you can never lower. These are estimates from the volume and ticket size on file, not a guarantee. Your Square sales summary makes them exact.
