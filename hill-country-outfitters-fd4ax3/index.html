Where We Are

You told me this year has cost you about $10,000 in stolen-card chargebacks, even with every filter turned on, and that you now call every customer before you ship. That works at your size today, but it will not keep up as you grow, and you should not have to do it. The piece your checkout is missing is 3-D Secure, an extra check where the buyer's own bank confirms they are the real cardholder. This setup adds it to every online order, and it takes your card fees to $0 while we are at it.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Stop eating stolen-card losses. On an order that passes the bank's check, a stolen-card dispute is the card bank's loss, not yours.

  • Keep it fair for your regular customers, the folks buying grain mills and grain to bake for their families.
  • No contract. If we do not earn it, you leave.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing

3-D Secure on every online order

Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.
