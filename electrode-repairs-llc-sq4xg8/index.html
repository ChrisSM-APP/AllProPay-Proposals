What We Found

1. Flat-rate pricing is the wrong fit for your card mix

About 58% of your monthly volume runs on debit cards. On BlockChyp's flat-rate pricing (2.3% + $0.10), debit transactions are charged the same as credit cards. But the wholesale cost of processing regulated debit is roughly 0.30%, not 2.3%. You are paying multiples of what those transactions actually cost the processor.

Our pricing is IC + 0.50% + $0.05 per transaction + $25/month. No tiered buckets, no invented fees — same markup on every card type. For your debit-heavy mix, that translates to an estimated blended effective rate of ~2.0% (including the monthly fee) versus your current 2.41%. The debit cards costing you 2.3% today would drop to under 1%.

2. What BlockChyp is doing right — and what they are not

Worth saying clearly: your statement has no junk fees. No PCI non-compliance fee, no monthly statement fee, no regulatory surcharge, no IRS reporting fee. That puts BlockChyp ahead of probably 80% of the processors we see. We are not going to manufacture pain that does not exist on this statement.

The trade-off, though, is that you cannot see what any individual card actually costs to process — only one blended rate. Without that visibility, there is no way to optimize, and no built-in path to lower fees as your volume grows. Interchange-plus shows you every line item, and as your business grows, only the wholesale interchange portion of your costs grows. Our markup stays flat.
