Why a hand-added fee is always on the wrong side of the rules

Visa and Mastercard say a card fee must be
Registered with your processor 30 days before you startNever done
Shown to the customer before they pay, and itemized on the receiptReceipt only
On credit cards only. Never on a debit or prepaid cardEvery card
Capped. Visa set the ceiling for a surcharge at 3% in April 2023Usually over

You cannot tell a debit card from a credit card by hand, and the app you key it into never registered the fee. So a fee you add yourself breaks at least two of these every single time, and usually three. It does not matter how long you have done it or how fair the number feels.
