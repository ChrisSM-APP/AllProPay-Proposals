What the card brands require, and what a hand-added fee does

Visa and Mastercard say a card fee must be
Registered with your processor 30 days before you startNot done
Shown to the customer before they pay, and itemized on the receiptReceipt only
On credit cards only. Never on a debit or prepaid cardEvery card
Capped. Visa set the ceiling at 3% in April 2023Usually over

A fee you add yourself fails every line, no matter how long you have done it or how fair the number feels.

Even Square says so

Square now has its own surcharge switch. Read what it allows: 3% maximum, credit cards only, in person only. Debit stays on you. A hand-written 5% on every card is outside even Square's own tool.

The way to do it right

Same idea, built the other way around. Your price is the card price, and cash pays less.

  • Both prices are shown before anyone pays. On the screen and on the receipt. Nothing to write in, nothing to explain.
  • The app or terminal does the math. Every card, including debit, with no line you have to add.
  • Your cost on card sales is $0. On AllPro Rebate, part of it comes back to you every month.
  • If a payment is ever disputed, you call a person who knows your account. Not an 800 number.
