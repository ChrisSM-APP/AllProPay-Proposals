If You Ever Want Your Card Cost At Zero

You told me you bury the card fee in your margin and you are not asking customers for it, and I am not going to push you on that. It is still worth knowing the option sits on the same account. Plenty of rental and service companies now show two prices, one for card and one for bank transfer or check, and their card cost goes to zero. Nothing about your app or your paperwork changes if you ever decide to turn it on, and it turns off just as easily. For now this proposal is a straight rate improvement and your customers see nothing different.

  • Start on true-cost pricing. Nothing your customers notice, and the rate comes down on day one.
  • Bank transfer is the quiet win. 0.75 percent capped at $10 on your monthly rentals and your bigger jobs, whenever a customer is happy to pay that way.
  • Two-price checkout is there later if you want it. Same account, same app, no new application. Your call, whenever, or never.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your app does not change. Our API sits underneath it, and your developer gets the documentation and a test environment before you move a single customer.
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.20 percent and 10 cents. That is the whole markup over the real cost of the card. No tiers, no padding.
  • You stop paying one flat 2.9 percent on debit. Debit and everyday cards cost a fraction of that at true cost, and on true-cost pricing you keep the difference.
  • Bank transfer at 0.75 percent, capped at $10. A $1,500 job costs $10 instead of about $36, and a $5,000 one still costs $10.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk. That matters more when you are about to double the business.
