Where We Are

You walked us through how Urban Energy actually gets paid, and it is simpler than most: the business runs on checks, and Square is really just the deposit lane. Invoices go out from the Square web platform, deposits land a few hundred dollars up to about a thousand, and on anything over a thousand you already add the card fee as a line item. The goal is not to change any of that. It is to stop paying for the part you are already handling by hand.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Checks stay exactly as they are. Most of your customers never touch a card and nothing about their experience changes.
  • Invoicing keeps working the way it works today, sent from your platform, paid online by the customer.
  • The card fee you already add on jobs over $1,000 becomes automatic and applies to every card, so nobody in the field has to make that call.
  • One flat monthly cost, no contract, and no equipment to rip out.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash, check, and ACH customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Ready for the tablets
You mentioned moving the crews onto tablets so they can take payment on site. The account is built for that from day one. Tap to pay works in the field the same way the invoice link works at the office, and both run on the same pricing, so the switch does not cost you a second setup or a second application.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You were straight with us that Square works fine for you today, so the account is built so you are never stuck with a decision you made in August.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee instead of passing it, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account with no reapplication.
  • Once the crews are on tablets and more of your volume is a tapped card in person, that interchange-plus option gets meaningfully better, because tapped debit is priced far below anything you can get on a flat rate. We will re-run it with you then.
  • Nothing is locked. Month to month, and the account moves with you.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your card cost on sales goes to $0. Your monthly is a flat $25, versus roughly $500 a month today.
  • You are already passing the fee above $1,000. This makes it automatic and consistent instead of a judgment call on each invoice.
  • Check-paying customers, which is most of your book, see no change at all.
  • No contract, no cancellation fee, and no hardware to migrate. You are on web invoicing today, so there is nothing to unplug.
  • The account is ready for the tablet rollout you are already planning, at no extra setup.

On a $900 deposit today, Square takes roughly $30 before the money reaches you. On Edge that $30 does not come out of the business. Across a normal month at your volume, that is the difference between paying about $500 and paying $25.
