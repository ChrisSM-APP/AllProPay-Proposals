The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked me directly whether there is a plan for an account that sits quiet most months, because Wells Fargo charges you $20 a month for a dealership terminal you barely touch. There is, and it is the reason I want both sides of your business on one account instead of two.

  • The dealership rides under the parts account. One application, one monthly fee, and the truck side carries no monthly charge of its own. A month with no card down payments costs you nothing extra.
  • Deposits still go where you want them. Parts money to the parts account, dealership money to the dealership account. Separate deposits, one processing relationship.
  • The dealership terminal is yours to use or ignore. If that side takes a card three times a year, fine. You are not paying a monthly fee to keep it sitting on the counter.
  • Nothing is locked. Month to month, no cancellation fee, and no automatic renewal waiting for you a year from now.
