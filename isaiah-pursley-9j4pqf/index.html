Here is the math on a small ticket. On credit, the adjustment is about 3.5%. It is shown on the screen before the customer pays. Tap to pay counts as credit.

  • $5 cone, paid in cash. The customer pays $5.00. You keep $5.00.
  • $5 cone, paid on credit or tap to pay. The customer pays about $5.18. You keep $5.00.
  • $10 sale, paid on credit or tap to pay. The customer pays about $10.35. You keep $10.00.

So your cost per card sale is $0. Your only fixed cost is the $25 a month. The exact posted credit rate will be printed on your application before you sign, so you can check it against this page.
