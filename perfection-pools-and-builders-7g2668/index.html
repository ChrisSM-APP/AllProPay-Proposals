Where We Are

Mike, this is everything we went through on the phone, in one place. You have built Perfection Pools without taking cards because you did not want a percentage of your own work going to a card company. That is a fair position and I am not asking you to change it. What I am showing you is how to start taking cards without that cost coming out of your money, and how to stop waiting on a check for a $2,000 job.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • No percentage of your work goes to a card company. The processing cost does not come out of your money.
  • Cash, check, and bank transfer stay exactly as they are, so every customer still has a way to pay the lower price.
  • A physical terminal, not your phone. You asked about that directly, and it is how the account is built.
  • You wanted to confirm we are a real company before anything else. Our address, phone number, and reviews are all on this page and all checkable.
  • Nothing signed, nothing upfront, and nothing to cancel if you decide it is not for you.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
Your posted price is the card price, and cash, check, and bank transfer customers get a discount off it. The processing cost is already covered before the sale happens, so on a card sale your business keeps effectively all of it.
A terminal, not your phone
You get a dedicated card terminal for the truck. It is a closed, encrypted device built to do one job, so your phone and everything on it stay out of the payment side completely. You key in the job amount, the terminal works out the card price itself, and the customer taps or inserts. No math on your end.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

On a $2,000 job the card price is about $76 higher than the cash price, and that difference is what covers the processing. You get the full $2,000, in your account the next morning. A customer who would rather pay cash or write you a check pays the lower price, the same as today. The only thing that changes is that you now have a way to say yes when somebody wants to hand you a card.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You have not decided how you want to take the card yet, and you do not have to decide today. The account carries all of it.

  • Start with the terminal. If you ever want to turn your phone into a reader later, it is already on the account. If you never turn it on, nothing is lost.
  • Invoice or text a link. For the jobs you bill out, send a payment link straight to the customer's phone instead of mailing an invoice and waiting on the post.
  • Use it on the jobs you want to. Nobody is asking you to stop taking cash or checks. Cards are an extra lane, not a replacement.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • You start accepting cards without a percentage of your work going to a card company.
