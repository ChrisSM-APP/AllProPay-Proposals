Clover vs Square vs AllProPay: the honest comparison

You asked for the cons of both so you can balance the scales with the owner group. Here it is straight, and it is all stuff any of you can verify. I am not here to trash anyone. I just want the whole picture on the table before your group signs a multi-year deal with anybody.

  • Clover: if the hardware is not bought outright it is usually a multi-year lease, often four years, and the terminals are locked to Clover and generally cannot be reprogrammed. So even if the rate climbs later, the equipment keeps you there. Expect an annual fee and a PCI fee on top, and those are set by the platform, not the friendly rep quoting you a rate.
  • Square: a flat 2.6 percent and 10 cents in person, closer to 2.9 percent and 30 cents on invoices, with margin baked in that you never see. You pay that same flat rate on a low-cost debit card as on a premium rewards card, so you overpay on the debit that runs through a food counter, and the flat rate only gets more expensive as you grow. Support is self-service with no named rep, and Square is known to hold or freeze funds on flagged accounts.
  • AllProPay: interchange, the true cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.5 percent and 8 cents a sale. No lease, no contract, no annual fee, no PCI fee, and a free terminal if you want one. A named rep sets it up and answers the phone, and you can walk anytime because there is nothing to cancel.
