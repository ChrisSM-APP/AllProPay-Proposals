Where We Are

Taylor, here is what you went through with Torin, in one place. Everything at The Blonde Bar happens in the chair and at the front desk, nothing online. You are on Square, and right now you absorb the card fee yourself. You were also straight with him about why you have stayed put: Square fronts you $10,000 to $20,000 when you need it, and you still have about $7,000 on the books. I am not going to ask you to walk away from that. So this page does two things. It shows you what the card fee is actually costing the salon, and it shows you how to stop paying it without disturbing the balance you owe.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • In store only. Every sale happens at the front desk, so your whole book sits on card-present pricing. That is the cleanest kind of account to move.
  • You absorb the fee today. At $12,000 to $20,000 a month on cards, that is roughly $3,800 to $6,400 a year coming out of service revenue.
  • The advance stays the advance. You owe that $7,000 either way. Nothing on this page pays it off, speeds it up, or asks you to close your Square account.
  • The front desk does not change. Same checkout, same tap or dip, same speed. The only difference is the price is shown two ways.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card price and the cash price are posted together at the front desk. Cash and check customers pay the lower cash price. Card customers pay the posted card price, which carries the processing cost. On a card sale your business keeps effectively all of it.
Built for the front desk
A countertop terminal that taps, dips, and swipes, with tips prompted on screen the way your team is used to. It arrives with your card price and cash price already loaded, so nobody at the desk has to do math or explain a policy they just learned.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

Square takes 2.6% plus a dime on every in-person sale, and because you absorb it, that comes straight off what the chair earns. On Edge the card price and the cash price are posted together and the client picks which one they pay. Your card cost goes to $0. On a $200 color service that is $208 on a card or $200 in cash, and you keep $200 either way. Today that same $200 card sale leaves about $5.30 with Square, every single time.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You stayed with Square for access to cash, not for the rate. That is a fair reason, and it is worth saying plainly that none of this has to be all or nothing.

  • Move at whatever pace protects the advance. We can open the account and bring volume across as your balance comes down, so repayment on your current advance is never starved. Before we set that pace, pull up your advance terms and find the minimum repayment, because most of these carry one and it decides the timing.
  • We work with business funding partners too. Once you are live and processing with us, we can look at funding from our side as well. No number and no promise until someone actually underwrites it, but it is a door that opens once there is processing history with us.
  • Change your mind whenever you want. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee again, we move you to flat interchange pricing on the same account, same terminal. No penalty either way.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your card cost goes to $0. The processing cost sits in the posted card price instead of coming out of what you earn on the service.
  • An estimated $3,800 to $6,400 a year stays in the salon, based on the volume you gave Torin.
  • Your advance is untouched. We do not need you to pay off Square, stop using them, or change anything about how that balance comes down.
  • $25 a month flat, terminal included. No contract, no setup fee, and no cancellation fee.

Run it across a normal month. At $16,000 in card sales you are handing Square roughly $427. On Edge that line is $0 and your only monthly cost is the $25 platform fee. The money does not come from cutting anything at the salon, it comes from the fee moving to the posted card price instead of your service revenue.
