What We See on Pre-Review

1. QuickBooks Payments flat rates are not optimized for HVAC card mix

QuickBooks charges one flat rate by entry method, regardless of what the card actually costs to process. A regulated debit card and a premium rewards card cost very different amounts at the network level, but you pay the same rate on both. With interchange-plus pricing, you only pay the true network cost plus a small fixed markup, so debit and regulated transactions cost less.

2. Invoiced and keyed transactions are where the money leaks

Invoiced card payments in QuickBooks cost 2.99 percent. Keyed transactions cost 3.5 percent. For an HVAC company that bills customers, dispatches techs, and takes phone payments, that is the bulk of the revenue and it is running at the highest rates on the card. Moving to interchange-plus or to a dual-pricing program is the single biggest lever.

3. The business is currently eating 100 percent of the fees

Roughly $24K to $32K a year in processing costs is being absorbed directly into the Wellers P&L. A dual-pricing model would let card-paying customers absorb the cost at the time of payment, while cash and check customers pay the listed price. Done correctly, this removes nearly all processing costs from the books.
