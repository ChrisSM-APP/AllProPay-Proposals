The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked to see the straight-rate option next to Edge, so here it is. Both are on the table and you can move between them on the same account.

  • AllProPay Edge is what I am recommending. Your cost to accept a card is $0 and the fee sits with the card-paying customer. On your volume that is the option that actually moves money.
  • AllProPay Rate is the straight-rate alternative if you would rather keep absorbing the fee yourself. You pay true interchange plus a flat markup instead of one blanket 2.66%, so your debit finally gets priced as debit. It is a real improvement over what you have, but I want to be straight with you: on your volume it is a smaller win than Edge.
  • You are not locked into the choice. If you start on Edge and decide you would rather absorb the fee, we move you to AllProPay Rate on the same account with no reapplication.
