What You Get
The counter stays simple
A countertop terminal that does exactly what you do now. You type in the amount, the customer taps, dips, or swipes, and it is done. You told us you run everything as a custom sale because card prices move daily and logging inventory is pointless, so nothing here asks you to build an item list or learn a system. The terminal is included in the monthly fee below. No lease and no equipment bill.
True-cost card pricing
You pay interchange, which is the real cost of the card set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.50 percent and 8 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup, and it is the same on every card brand including American Express. A flat rate charges you one price on every card, so a debit card that costs a fraction of a percent to run gets billed at the same rate as a premium rewards card. On true-cost pricing you keep that difference instead of your processor keeping it.
No junk fees, and a person who answers
No PCI fee, no statement fee, no annual fee, no batch fee, no downgrade penalties. Keeping you PCI compliant is part of the service, not a line on your bill. You get my direct number rather than a call center, and if a chargeback ever lands I handle the paperwork with you.
