Where We Are

You told me you pay 1.4 percent and no other fees, and when I asked whether that sat on top of what Visa and Mastercard charge, you said yes. That answer is the whole reason I am writing this. It means the 1.4 percent is not your rate, it is your processor's markup, and a markup is the one part of the bill that is negotiable. Here is what I would do with it.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Your 1.4 percent is the markup, not the rate. Underneath it sits interchange, the true cost Visa and Mastercard set on every card. Interchange is the same number for me as it is for the company you use now. Nobody discounts it. The markup on top is the only piece anyone controls, and yours is roughly double what a shop your size should be paying.
  • You do not take American Express or Discover. Nothing here asks you to change that. It does mean your card mix is clean Visa and Mastercard, which is the easiest kind of book to price honestly.
  • You said everyone is looking to save. Agreed, so I am not going to dress this up. There is one number moving on this page and it is the markup.
  • Nothing about how you run the counter has to change. Same way of taking a card, same day to day. This is a pricing change, not a workflow project.

Your Second Option: AllProPay Edge

AllProPay Rate is what I am recommending, because it cuts your cost without changing anything your customers see. But there is a second option worth knowing about, and some shops prefer it. AllProPay Edge takes your card cost to zero instead of cutting it in half. You post one price, that posted price is the card price, and customers paying cash or check pay less than posted. The card-paying customer covers the processing, so the cost comes off your books entirely rather than shrinking. It is the same account and the same application, so you can start on AllProPay Rate and move to Edge later, or never, without reapplying.

  • Edge takes your card cost to $0 rather than cutting it from 1.4 to 0.70. On the volumes in the table above that is the difference between keeping a few thousand a year and keeping all of it.
  • You start on AllProPay Rate and switch whenever you want. Same account, same equipment, no new paperwork and no new underwriting. Nothing about picking one now locks you out of the other.
  • Debit can be carved out so debit customers pay the lower posted price too. Most shops that run Edge keep it to credit only.
  • The reason I am not leading with it is that Edge changes what your customers see at the counter and AllProPay Rate does not. On a shop like yours with repeat customers who know your pricing, that is a real consideration and it is your call, not mine. Happy to walk through how other shops handle it.
