Your Current Setup

You are using QuickBooks Payments for your invoicing with a cash discount program in place, which passes the processing fees to your customers. That is a smart structure - you are already doing one of the most important things right.

The question is not whether the structure works. It is whether the platform you are using to run it is the right fit long term. QuickBooks is excellent accounting software. Payment processing is a different product, and bundling the two is not always the most cost-effective way to run either one.

Where AllProPay Fits

Three areas where we typically improve on a QuickBooks Payments setup:

  • A compliant program - not surcharging What QuickBooks sets up is technically surcharging - the fee is added on top and pulled from your account after the transaction. That does not meet Visa and Mastercard's requirements for a properly disclosed cash discount or dual-pricing program. AllProPay sets up a compliant program from the start, structured correctly for card-brand rules, which removes your exposure.
  • More ways to take payments QuickBooks Payments is invoicing-only. With AllProPay you can take in-person payments on a terminal, send a customer a payment link, accept payments through your website, or text someone a link on the spot. Same cash discount program, more ways to get paid.
  • Your QuickBooks stays exactly the same We sync directly with QuickBooks so your accounting workflow does not change. Invoices, reconciliation, reporting - all of it stays in QuickBooks. You are just running a better payment program behind the scenes.
