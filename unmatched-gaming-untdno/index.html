Built for Release Weekends and Tournament Days

You told me the store does not run on an average day. A 100-person tournament is a few hundred taps at $15 to $20 each. A release weekend is boxes at $300 and cases at $4,000, and a $10,000 day is normal. A flat rate with a 15-cent tap fee punishes both ends: the tournament day on the taps, the release weekend on the percentage. One rate that follows the true cost of the card handles both without you thinking about it.

  • Tournament days: 5 cents a tap instead of 15, on every entry, every booster, every re-buy.
  • Release weekends: a $300 box or a $4,000 case costs the true rate of the card plus 0.40 percent, not a flat 2.6 percent whatever the card.
  • Slow months: the pricing is the same. No tiers to fall out of, no minimum volume to hit.
