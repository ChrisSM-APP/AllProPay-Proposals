Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.35 percent and 10 cents. That is the whole markup over the true cost of the card. No tiers, no padding, no surprise fees in month six.
  • You stop paying 2.6 percent on debit and everyday cards. Those cost a fraction of that at true cost, and on true-cost pricing you keep the difference.
  • Rewards cards are handled honestly. A premium rewards card lands around what you pay today. It is never the reason to stay on a flat rate, because the flat rate charges you that price on every other card too.
  • Bank payments on the big tickets. A customer paying a spa invoice by bank transfer costs you 0.75 percent, capped at $10. A $6,000 tub paid by bank costs $10, not $156.
  • Same setup, no contract, no cancellation fee. Countertop terminal, QR and invoice payments, month to month. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.

Here is the math on a $15,000 month, the middle of the range you gave me. On a flat 2.6 percent you pay about $390 in card fees, and that same 2.6 percent is charged on debit cards that cost a fraction of it to run. Add a few customers paying a spa invoice by bank at $10 a pop and the gap widens again. These are estimates from the volume you shared, not a guarantee.
