What You Get
Shopify stays
You keep the store you have. Authorize.net is one of the payment gateways Shopify supports, so it connects to Shopify as your payment provider and our processing sits behind it. Orders, checkout, and delivery run the way they do today. The money just settles to you at true cost. Cards taken in the shop run on the same account and the same markup.
True-cost pricing
You pay interchange, the real cost set by Visa, Mastercard, and the card brands, plus a flat 0.20 percent and 15 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup, and it is the same on every card. Stripe charges one flat 2.9 percent plus 30 cents on every online order, whatever the card. Most cards cost far less than that to run. On true-cost pricing you keep the difference.
Real support, no lock-in
A named rep who connects the gateway to your Shopify store, answers the phone, and handles any chargeback paperwork with you. One flat monthly fee covers the gateway, PCI, statements, and support. No annual fee, no setup fee, no contract, no cancellation fee.

Online, in the Shop, and Over the Phone

You asked for the fee structure for both in-person and remote payments. It is one structure. The markup is the same whether a customer taps a card at the counter on 28th Street, checks out on the website, or calls in an order: interchange plus 0.20 percent and 15 cents. The only thing that moves is interchange itself, and it is lower on a card that is physically present, so in-shop sales land below the online estimate on this page.

  • Online: Authorize.net connected to Shopify. Checkout does not change.
  • In the shop: the same account and the same markup, at card-present interchange, which runs lower.
  • Phone orders: keyed on the same account, same markup. One statement for all of it.
