The Flexibility You Asked For

Dual pricing is the recommended setup because it takes the fee off your plate completely. If you ever decide you would rather not show a separate card price, you can switch to a straight, low-rate program where Evergreen absorbs the fee at a clean, transparent rate instead. Same account, same workflow, your call either way.

  • Start on dual pricing now. Your card fee drops to zero from day one.
  • Switch to a flat low rate whenever you want. Same account, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • No lock-in. Try one, see it on your statement, and move to the other if it fits better. No penalty either way.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • The fee you absorb today goes back to Evergreen. Roughly $1,500 to $1,800 a year stays in the business instead of going to card processing.
  • The price is shown up front. Your customer sees the card price at checkout and chooses, so there are no surprises and nothing to argue about later.
  • It is built the right way. A properly structured, clearly disclosed program that follows the card brand rules, not a fee quietly added after the sale.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If it is not a fit, you walk. No penalty.
  • No heavy lifting on your end. It plugs into how you already invoice. No month-by-month fee audits, no big system change.

Here is the math on a real job: on a $200 invoice, the card price shows as about $206 to $208 at checkout. Your customer sees both the card price and the cash price and picks. You collect the full $200 either way, and the processing fee is covered. Run that across a typical month and the roughly $150 you give up today stays with Evergreen.
