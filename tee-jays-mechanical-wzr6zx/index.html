The Flexibility You Asked For

Two things worth knowing up front. First, every claim in this proposal is a claim we will put on paper, including the cancellation coverage. If any rep, ours or anyone else's, will not put a number in writing, that tells you what the number is worth. Second, if you ever want your card cost near zero, Alberta allows a program where the card fee moves to the card-paying customer while cash and Interac debit customers pay list price. Same account, same terminal, no new application. Most shops start on interchange plus and keep that option in their back pocket.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today.
