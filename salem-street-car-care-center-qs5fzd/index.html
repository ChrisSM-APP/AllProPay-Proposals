The Flexibility You Asked For

A couple of things here are yours to set, not mine, and I would rather you picked them than have me pick for you.

  • You do not have to jump straight to 4%. You set the split. Post the full 4% and your cost is $0 and the rebate is on. Or leave your customers exactly where they are at 3% and cover the small remainder yourself. Or anywhere in between. The rebate is built on the 4% rate, so that is the one I would pick for you, but the account works either way and it can be changed later without new paperwork.
  • How much comes back. 0.25% is the standard tier at 4% posted. There is a step up that pays 0.40%, and the posted rate goes to 4.5%. On a repair order that is a real difference at the counter, so it is worth deciding with your volume in front of both of us rather than in a proposal.
  • If you ever decide you would rather carry the fee yourself again, we move you to straight interchange-plus on the same account. No reapplication, no new paperwork.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If we stop earning the business you walk.
