Your current setup

Lifestyles Sports runs two separate payment processors today - Priority Payment Systems handling the in-store volume through Lane 7000 terminals, and Stripe handling online orders through BigCommerce. Each processor has its own rate structure, its own fee schedule, and its own billing.

The problem with running two processors is that most merchants focus on the rate they know - in your case, the 1.75% from Priority Pay - and don't always factor in what the second processor is actually costing them. On a combined volume of $160,000 a month, that second number matters.

Stripe on BigCommerce: the expensive channel

Stripe's standard rate for ecommerce transactions is 2.90% plus $0.30 per transaction. On $60,000 a month in online volume, that works out to roughly $1,740 in processing fees plus an estimated $120 in per-transaction charges - approximately $1,860 a month just for the online side.

That's more than double the rate you're paying on your in-store volume, on a channel that represents over a third of your total business.

The 1.75% you think you're paying is only half the picture. When Stripe is added in, your actual blended rate across both channels is approximately 2.26%. On $160,000 a month, that difference is real money - roughly $43,300 a year going out the door in processing fees.
What AllProPay would do differently

The simplest and most immediate move is to replace Stripe on your BigCommerce store with Authorize.net, the payment gateway AllProPay works with. This is a configuration change inside BigCommerce - not a platform switch, not a rebuild. BigCommerce has native, built-in support for Authorize.net. Our team handles the setup.

Your in-store setup - the Lane 7000 terminals, your Priority Pay relationship - stays exactly as is for now. We start with the channel that is costing you the most and address it first.

For the online channel, we price on an interchange-plus model. That means you pay the actual interchange rate set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a transparent, fixed markup. No bundled rates, no rate games. What you sign for is what you pay.
