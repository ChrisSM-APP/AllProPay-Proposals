Where We Are

Joey, this is the revised version you asked for on Friday, and it is written so you can hand it straight to Randy without having to explain any of it first. Integrity Auto runs on Tekmetric, the payments behind it run on Stripe, and the shop pays the card fee out of its own money on every job. You told me plainly that you would rather pass that on, and that Tekmetric and Stripe give you no way to do it. That is the whole reason this page exists. Tekmetric does not move. You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Tekmetric does not move. POS+ runs inside your Tekmetric dashboard as a widget. The repair order, the invoice, and the payment stay in one flow, with no double entry.
  • Text-to-pay works the way it does now. The customer gets a link on the invoice and pays from their phone. The fee is already built into the link, so nothing is collected by hand afterwards.

  • Stripe stays where it is. It is native to Tekmetric. If you ever switch us off, Stripe takes back over on its own.

The piece that matters most for the invoiced half is the covered card rate. When you invoice today you are paying about 3.5% yourself, and it does not actually cover everything. A rewards card or an Amex costs more than a flat rate collects, so the difference quietly comes off your side. Our covered rate sits at 3.8461%, and it is built to cover every card your customers hand you. There is no card you can take through the shop where you get back less than 100% of the sale. That is the difference between a flat rate that mostly covers it and a program that actually does.
