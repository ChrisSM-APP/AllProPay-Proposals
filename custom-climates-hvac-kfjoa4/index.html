What You Get
Keep Housecall Pro, we connect it (this is POS+)
POS+ is the piece that links Housecall Pro straight to AllProPay. Your team keeps working in Housecall Pro exactly as they do now, same screens, same invoices, same card on file. POS+ sits behind it and routes each card payment to AllProPay instead of Stripe, so the only thing that changes is the rate you pay. There is nothing new to learn and no second app to run at the counter or in the truck. That $40 a month covers the POS+ connection.
Real pricing, off the flat rate
Stripe charges Housecall Pro merchants one flat rate near 3 percent on every card. You move to interchange, the true cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat half percent and 10 cents a transaction. That flat markup is the whole story: no tiers, no padding. On your volume, a setup like yours blends around 2.2 to 2.6 percent all-in against the roughly 3 percent you run today.
A real person, and no contract
You get a named rep who answers the phone, sets the Housecall Pro connection up the way you want it, and handles any chargeback paperwork with you. No contract and no cancellation fee. Tammy is also filing the closure on the old North account so the $70 a month stops, whether or not you move forward here.
