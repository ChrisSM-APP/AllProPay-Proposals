What We Are Cleaning Up

On a real dual-pricing program, the merchant should not be paying processing fees on top of the customer paying them. The fact that your current provider has been billing you anyway, in fluctuating amounts, is the issue. Switching to AllProPay closes that gap.

2. Predictability

One of the things you mentioned was not knowing what the bill was going to be from month to month. Our $25 platform fee is flat. It does not move with volume, card mix, or anything else. The only number that ever changes on your statement is your sales.

3. Direct support

You get my direct line and the account manager's direct line. Anything on the account, billing questions, equipment, statements, you call us, not a 1-800 queue. That is how we work with every merchant we bring on.
