Where You Are Today

Gabriel, you have built something real. Square does a decent job on the everyday flow, you are already passing the card fee to your customers, and your sales feed straight into QuickBooks. That part works.

What does not work is the part you only feel when something goes wrong. You had a dispute go all the way to court. And even though you pass the fee, Square still takes its cut on top. This page is about fixing those two things without changing the way you run the shop day to day.

Here is what we heard matters most to you:

  • Keep the all-in-one feel: estimates, invoices, text or email to pay, and tap in person, all in one place.
  • Real dispute protection. You should not have to go to court to fight a chargeback alone.
  • Keep 100% of every sale. If your customer is already covering the fee, none of it should be skimmed off the top.
  • Room to grow: add your techs as users with their own permissions, and set up recurring or deposit billing for bigger jobs.
  • Stay connected to QuickBooks so your books do not change.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are already passing the fee, so cash discount fits how you run today and the switch is invisible to your customers. But you are not locked into it.

  • Start on cash discount now. Keep 100% of the sale, just like you intend to today, with no processor cut on top.
  • Switch to flat low pricing whenever you want. Same account, same app, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run it, look at a real statement, and decide. No penalty either way.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • You already pass the fee, so moving to cash discount is invisible to your customers. The difference is you stop letting Square take a cut on top.
  • The dispute that put you in court would have a dedicated rep and a bank-backed risk team behind it next time.
  • Estimate to invoice to paid, in one flow, plus recurring and deposit billing for the bigger wrap and PPF jobs.
  • Add your techs as users with their own permissions as you hire, with admin controls you keep.
