The Option When You Are Ready

You mentioned the idea of not charging customers for debit. There is a compliant way to do it, and it lives on the same account, so you can flip to it whenever you want with no new paperwork.

  • Surcharge credit only. Credit cards carry the fee, and we can match your current rate so your customers see no change.
  • Debit billed to the business at 1% plus 20 cents. A much lower rate than treating debit like credit. This part lands on you, not the customer.
  • No rush, no commitment. Most shops your size start on the flat 3% above and leave it there. The split is there if you ever want it, on the same terminal and the same account.

Our honest read for a shop your size: start with the flat 3% and no monthly fee. It saves you money day one and asks nothing of you or your customers. Keep the debit split in your back pocket.
