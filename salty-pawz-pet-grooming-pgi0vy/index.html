Where We Are

Right now you are on Square, and you are the one paying the card fees: a flat 2.6 percent and 10 cents on every sale. Here is the problem with a flat rate for an all-in-person shop like yours. You pay that same 2.6 percent on debit and everyday cards that cost a fraction of that to run, so you overpay on a big share of your sales. The 10 cents on every swipe adds up fast on smaller grooming tickets. And you never see the real cost of the card, just Square's one bundled rate that you cannot lower. The goal is simple. Keep checkout exactly as easy as it is today, and stop paying that fee out of your own pocket.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:
