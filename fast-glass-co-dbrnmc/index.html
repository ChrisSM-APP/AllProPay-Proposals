The Short Version

The headline you raised on our calls: your techs hate manually editing invoices because they do not know up front whether the customer is paying cash or card. That manual decision lives at the end of every job and it eats time, and when it goes wrong it eats the surcharge revenue too.

This proposal removes that step entirely. HouseCall Pro stays. Every estimate, job, technician workflow, and in-field card processing continues to run exactly as it does today. We add one extension called POS+ that sits inside HouseCall Pro. The surcharge is calculated and applied automatically at the moment the invoice is created. The customer pays however they want to pay. HouseCall Pro marks the invoice "Paid" and logs the transaction ID on its own.

On the processing side, Stripe gets replaced in the backend by AllProPay's Edge program. Your effective processing rate drops to 0% because the compliant card surcharge passes that cost to the cardholder. At your monthly volume, that puts $10,000 to $27,000 a year back in the business.
