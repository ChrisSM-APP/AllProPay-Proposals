Where We Are

Roy, you told us straight that this is not about the rate. You would rather throw $3,000 away than go through another vendor switch, and after a bad one and four years without a day off, that is a fair position to take. So this is not a rate sheet. It is the migration plan: exactly what we do, exactly what you do, how long it takes, and what happens if it goes wrong. The money is at the bottom of the page, where it belongs.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • The headache is the problem, not the price. Any switch has to be something we do, not something you do. You are already running the business without a day off.
  • Everything has to come across intact. Your inventory, your 40 to 50 services with their prices, your payment links, your invoices, and your purchase orders. If they do not come across clean, there is no point doing this.
  • No surcharging, no dual pricing, no cash discount. You decided your customers are not paying a fee at checkout. That is a customer-experience call, it is yours, and we are not going to argue with you about it.
  • You already own about $10,000 of hardware. We are not going to pretend that away or bury it in a footnote. You get an honest, written answer on every piece of it before you commit to anything.
  • Nothing gets switched off until you say so. No cutover weekend, no day where you cannot take money.
What You Get
We do the migration, not you
We export your item list, your 40 to 50 services with their prices, and your customer records, and we rebuild them on the new account ourselves. You re-key nothing. When it is built we hand you a login and you check it against your live system, side by side, before anything moves.
Both systems run until you say switch
Nothing gets turned off. The new account sits beside what you run today while you put a handful of real transactions through it, including a payment link and an invoice. Only when you are satisfied that inventory, services, invoices, and POs all behave do you move your volume. If it is not right, you stay exactly where you are.
One person, on the phone, the whole way
You get a named rep and a direct number, not a support queue and not a new person every call. Same person for the build, for go-live day, and for the first month after. No contract and no cancellation fee, so if we make your life harder you leave and it costs you nothing.
