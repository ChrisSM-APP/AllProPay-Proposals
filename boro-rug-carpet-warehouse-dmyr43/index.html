The Flexibility You Asked For

There is a second way to run this if you ever want it. Instead of absorbing the card fee, you can pass it to the card-paying customer, the way a lot of shops do now, which brings your card cost close to zero. It is the same account and the same equipment, no new application. Most owners at your volume start on the lower flat rate and keep the pass-through option in their back pocket. We can walk through both.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today.
  • Switch to dual pricing whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run a few months on IC+, see the actual cost on your statement, and decide if you want to move it to the customer. No penalty either way.
