Where We Are

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Omega EDI does not move. It runs your parts, VIN lookups, insurance billing, and job status. We point the payment side at Authorize.net or NMI, which Omega already lists in its own drop-down, and everything else stays where it is.

Terminals that work in the field, and a person who answers

Room To Move Later

You said you want to absorb the card cost yourself rather than put it on the customer, and this proposal is built that way. What you are not doing is locking yourself in. The account is set up to flex as the business does, and none of it requires a new application or new paperwork.

  • Add vans and terminals as you grow. Same account, same pricing, no re-application.
  • Keep a buy-now-pay-later option for the customers who need one. Tell me how you want that to work and I will get you a straight answer on it before you sign anything.
