Where We Are

Paul, when we spoke you told us three things that matter. You are on Square, almost every card gets keyed in over the phone, and you pay the whole processing fee out of your own pocket rather than put it on your customers. This page takes you at your word on that last part. Instead of moving the fee, it shrinks it: you keep paying it, but you pay the true cost of the card plus a small flat markup instead of Square's one-size-fits-all 3.5 percent.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Nothing about how you take a payment changes. You key the card in the same way, repeat customers sit on card on file, and receipts go out automatically.
  • Your customers see nothing new. No surcharge, no dual pricing, no signage. Your prices stay your prices, which is exactly how you told us you want it.
  • Your Square payroll stays exactly where it is. This only changes who processes the card, nothing else you run.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.
What You Get
Built for phone orders
Your AllProPay virtual terminal is made for exactly how you work: key the card in from the office, keep repeat customers on card on file, and send a payment link by text or email to anyone who would rather not read a card number over the phone. Funded next business day.
True-cost pricing, no tiers
Square charges you a flat 3.5 percent plus 15 cents on every keyed card because flat is easy to advertise. On interchange-plus you pay what the card actually costs, the rate set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.6 percent and 5 cents. When a customer hands you a cheap debit card, you keep the difference instead of Square.
Real support behind you
A named rep who answers the phone, sets the account up the way you want it, and handles any chargeback paperwork with you. You are not routing through a help center chat window.

If You Ever Change Your Mind About the Fee

You told us you would rather carry the card fee yourself than put it on your customers, and this proposal respects that. But you should know what the other lever is worth, because it is the bigger dollar move.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Transparent, lower than Square, and invisible to your customers.
  • The bigger move exists if you ever want it. Our Edge program moves the card cost to the card-paying customer, cash and check pay list price, and your card cost goes to an estimated $0. On your volume that is several thousand dollars a year. It is a different philosophy than the one you told us you hold, so it is here as an option, not the recommendation.
  • Same account either way. If you ever switch, it is a settings change, not a new application.
