Where the Friction Is

1. Clover and QuickBooks Are Not Connected

Every card sale processed through Clover requires a second, manual entry in QuickBooks. Nothing syncs automatically. At $5,000 to $10,000 in monthly volume across dozens of transactions, that is a lot of manual entries - each one a chance for a number to go in wrong or not at all. Commerce Sync (or a similar integration plugin) can push Clover data directly into QuickBooks automatically, eliminating the double-entry entirely. This is not a workaround - it is a standard integration that North-supported Clover setups handle cleanly.

2. No Way to Send a Payment Link

Right now every card payment requires either a physical Clover terminal or a phone call to manually run the card. For a mobile service business, that means customers cannot pay on their own time after a job is done - there is no "pay by text" or "pay by email" option. That creates follow-up calls, delayed collection, and more time spent chasing payment. North through AllProPay supports text and email payment links natively, so you can send an invoice link the moment a job is complete and let the customer pay when it is convenient for them.

3. Paper Receipts Creating a Bookkeeping Backlog

Without an automated sync between Clover and QuickBooks, receipts have to be held and manually matched to accounting entries. As Vincent described it, the business is bigger now and the bookkeeping has not kept pace. This is a direct result of two systems that do not talk to each other. Fixing the integration (Finding 1) eliminates the receipt problem at the same time - once Clover data flows into QuickBooks automatically, there is nothing left to reconcile manually.
