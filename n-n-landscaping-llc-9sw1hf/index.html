The Flexibility You Asked For

You told me you have not fully decided whether to pass the fee. That is fine, because the account is built to work either way, and you can change your mind after it is open.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you decide you would rather keep covering the fee yourself, we move you to AllProPay Rate on the same account, no reapplication: interchange (the true card cost set by Visa and Mastercard) plus a flat 0.60%.
  • On AllProPay Rate at your volume, the all-in typically lands around $150 to $185 a month against the roughly $180 you pay now. A one-statement review pins the exact number before you commit to anything.
  • Bank-transfer payments cost 0.50% capped at $10 instead of the 1% capped at $20 you pay on QuickBooks. That one saves you money on either program.
