What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On Edge the shop posts two prices: the cash price you would charge anyway, and a card price beside it. Anyone paying cash or writing a check pays exactly what they pay now. A customer who chooses to put it on a card covers the card price, and your cost on that sale is $0. The shop deposits the cash price either way.
Walk-ins at the counter, fleet customers on an invoice
The terminal sits at the counter for the customer who drives in and pays before they leave. For the fleet and commercial work you bill out, the same account sends an invoice the customer can pay from their phone, so you are not waiting on a check in the mail. It is one account and one $25 a month, whether the money comes in at the counter or off an invoice. I set it up with you over the phone, and if anything ever feels off you call me directly.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

Here is the thing worth knowing before you open a merchant account anywhere. On a standard account the shop pays the processing, and it runs around 3 percent of every card sale. At the $20,000 a month you mentioned, that is about $600 a month, or $7,200 a year, out of the business for the privilege of getting paid. That cost is real and it is the reason a lot of shops your size stay on cash. On Edge the card price is posted next to the cash price, the card customer covers the difference, and your cost on card sales is $0. Your only cost is a flat $25 a month for the whole account, terminal included. The volume above is an illustration, since you are not taking cards yet and you told me you were not sure. Once you have a real month behind you I will replace it with your number. And none of it counts the customers you are turning away today because they did not bring cash.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You told me you are not ready to decide today and that your wife runs the books. Nothing here asks you to commit before you are both comfortable, and the account is built so a slow month never becomes a problem.

  • Try it for a month or two. That is what I offered on the phone and it stands. If it does not bring you business, tell me and we close it. No penalty, no cancellation fee, nothing owed.
  • A slow month costs you $25, not a percentage. Because the shop pays nothing on card sales, a quiet month never turns into a big processing bill. That is the opposite of how a standard account behaves when business is soft.

  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. If this does not earn its place at the shop, you walk.
