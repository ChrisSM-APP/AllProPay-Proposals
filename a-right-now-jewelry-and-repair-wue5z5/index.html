The Flexibility You Asked For

You asked to see both ways of doing this so you could decide. Here they both are, and here is my honest read on which one is worth your time.

  • Edge is the one I would pick for you. Your card cost drops to zero and the fee sits on the card sale. At your volume this is the version where the numbers actually move.
  • Interchange-plus is the other way, and I will be straight with you about it. You would keep absorbing the fee, but pay the true network cost plus a small fixed markup instead of Square's flat 2.6%. At $2,000 to $5,000 a month the rate savings alone are small, and once a monthly fee is in the mix it lands close to a wash against Square. It is a real option and it is on the table. It is just not where the money is for a shop your size.
  • You are not locked into the choice. It is the same account and the same terminal either way, so if you start on one and want the other later, we move you without a new application.
