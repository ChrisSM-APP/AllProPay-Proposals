What You Get
Odoo stays, Stripe goes
Your website, your inventory, your POS screen, your loyalty, your contacts, and your books all stay in Odoo. We change one setting: the payment provider behind the checkout. On the store side you add a standalone smart terminal next to the PC and cash drawer you already use. Nothing gets rebuilt and nothing gets migrated.
True-cost card pricing
You pay interchange, the real cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.5 percent and 10 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup, and it is the same on every card brand including American Express. Stripe charges you one flat price on every card, including debit, where the true cost is a fraction of that. On true-cost pricing you keep the difference. The 30 cents Stripe takes on every online order goes away too.
One account, one person to call
The store and the website run on the same account, one monthly statement, one named rep. That is me. I handle the Authorize.Net setup inside Odoo with you, get the terminal to you configured, and deal with any chargeback paperwork myself. No contract and no cancellation fee, so if we stop earning it you walk.

Where This Goes as You Grow

You told me the store is new and the numbers are still low, and that is actually the argument for moving now rather than later. A flat rate never changes. It is 2.9 percent at $10,000 a month and it is still 2.9 percent at $50,000 a month. True-cost pricing moves with you: the interchange underneath is what it is, but the markup on top is a number you and I can revisit as the volume grows, and the per-sale cents matter less every month. Getting the plumbing right now also means you are not re-doing your Odoo payment setup in a year when the store is busy.

  • The store and the website sit on one account, one statement, one rate card. You are not running two relationships.
  • The markup is a number we can revisit as your volume grows. A flat rate is not.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee, so moving now costs you nothing if it does not work out.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • You are not locked into Stripe by Odoo. Authorize.Net is already on Odoo's supported provider list. This is a settings change on your site, not a development project.
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.5 percent and 10 cents. That is the whole markup over the true cost of the card, on every brand including American Express. No tiers and no padding.
  • You stop paying a flat price on debit. Stripe charges 2.7 percent at the counter whether the customer hands over a debit card or a premium rewards card. Debit costs a fraction of that to run, and on true-cost pricing you keep the difference.
  • The 30 cents per online order goes away. On your smaller web orders that per-sale charge is a bigger share of the cost than most people realise.
  • The terminal is included and there is no contract. No lease on the hardware, no setup fee, no cancellation fee. One monthly fee covers the terminal, the gateway, PCI, and support.
