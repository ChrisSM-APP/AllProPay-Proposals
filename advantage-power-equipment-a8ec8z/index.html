The program

Edge · Pass-through processing

The 3.5% goes to the customer, not your bank account.
  • Invoicing & text-to-pay: Exact same flow you use today. Convert estimate, hit send, customer gets both an SMS and an email link with your logo, they enter the card themselves.
  • Card fee handling: Add the line, send the invoice, the surcharge is calculated and disclosed automatically. You never do the math.
  • QuickBooks sync: Every paid transaction posts back to QuickBooks as paid. You match the customer name on your end. One-way sync.
  • Funding: Next-day to your bank account. Daily settlement, no manual reconciliation games.
  • Cost: $25 / month flat. No processing fees out of your account. No contract. Cancel any time.
  • Support: You call me directly. Matt Butcher, AllProPay. Not a call center in India.

Optional add-on: Hyfin (deeper QuickBooks integration)

For when you want everything to run natively inside QuickBooks instead of a separate app.
  • Plugin that runs AllProPay processing directly inside the QuickBooks interface

  • My recommendation: start with the standard Edge setup first. If the side-app workflow bothers you after 30 days, we add Hyfin then. Easy to layer in later. Harder to justify upfront when the standard setup already solves the funding-hold and slush-account problem.
