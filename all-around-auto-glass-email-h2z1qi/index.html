Where We Are

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

You Can Switch Between Them

You do not have to get this right today, and you are not stuck once you pick. Both options run on the same account, the same terminal, and the same invoicing. If you start on Option A and later decide you want your card cost at zero, I move you across. No new application, no new equipment, and nothing changes in how you work.

  • Start on Option A. Lower cost on the invoices you already send, plus a terminal ready for the day the showroom opens.
  • Move to Option B whenever you want. Same account, same terminal. Your card cost goes to zero and the disclosed rate rides on the card price.
  • Or run both. Option B at the counter where the customer sees the price before they pay, Option A on invoices and on anything an insurer settles. Tell me if insurers pay you by card and I will set it up that way.
