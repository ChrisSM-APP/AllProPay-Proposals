The soft number is transfer-to-close, which is a closer-and-lead-quality question, not an opener-volume one. That is the strategic thing for you and Tammy to own.

The two calls to make

1 · The platform switch (RingCentral/HubSpot → JustCall/Zoho)

Keep it. Dial efficiency is clearly up; the conversion dip is leads and focus.

The two openers who worked both systems dial faster on JustCall: John 106 → 135 calls per phone-hour +27%, Kurt 74 → 98 +32%. The tool is not the bottleneck.

The conversion softness lines up with the new-lead test and, on the 514 desk, with focus and hours, not the platform. Fix leads and close-rate separately.

2 · Kurt's role

Decide whether he is an opener or a trainer. Today he is billed as one and paid like the other.

His own desk slid (transfers 23 → 15.5/mo, only 21% of paid hours on the phone) while the 611 floor he coaches went 8.8% → 13.6% and hit 20% in June. The training may well be worth it, but you cannot score him as a full opener while he trains.

Pick the lane and set the matching target. His card lays this out for the 1-on-1.
