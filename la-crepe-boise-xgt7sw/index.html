Compliance exposure

Adding a percentage to a transaction as a workaround surcharge - without a card-brand-approved dual pricing or cash discount program - violates Visa and Mastercard operating rules. Square does not offer a compliant program for this. Most merchants do this for years without issue, but the exposure is real and can result in penalties or forced termination by the card brands. AllProPay sets this up the right way.

The AllProPay Solution

We recommend a dual pricing / cash discount program. Here is how it works:

  • Your menu shows two prices: a cash price and a card price.
  • Customers who pay by card pay 3.0% - built into the displayed price.
  • Customers who pay cash pay the base price.
  • You collect $0 in processing fees. The customer covers the cost transparently.
  • The program is fully compliant with Visa, Mastercard, and card-brand operating rules.
