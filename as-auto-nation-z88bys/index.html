What You Get
Your counter, plus online invoicing
Nothing changes at the counter. What gets added is online invoicing, so you can send a customer a payment link by email or text and collect a deposit or a balance before the car moves. Card on file for anyone paying in stages. I set the account up with you and stay on the phone until the first payment lands.
True-cost pricing, not one bundled rate
You pay interchange, the real cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.50 percent and 5 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup. Bank of America bundles everything into one rate, so a $2,000 debit down payment is billed at the same 2.5 percent as a premium rewards card, even though a debit card costs a fraction of that to run. On true-cost pricing you keep that difference instead of your bank keeping it.
Real support, no lock-in
A named rep who answers the phone, gets the account set up the way you want it, and handles any chargeback paperwork with you. No contract, no setup fee and no cancellation fee.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are absorbing the card fee today and this proposal keeps it that way, because that is what you asked for. It is worth knowing the account is not locked into one way of pricing. If you ever want your card cost to go to zero by posting a cash price and a card price, the same account switches over. No new application, no new equipment, and nothing you have to decide now.

  • Either way there is no contract, so you are never stuck with the choice.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Interchange plus a flat 0.50 percent and 5 cents a transaction. That is the whole markup over the true cost of the card. No tiers, no padding, and you can see every line.
  • You stop paying one bundled rate on debit. A $2,000 debit down payment costs you about $50 at 2.5 percent today. At true cost that same card runs for a small fraction of it. This is where most of your money is sitting.
  • Blended, a book like yours lands around 1.9 to 2.4 percent all in, against about 2.5 percent today, and lower again in a month with a lot of debit.
  • Online invoicing is included. Send a payment link by email or text, hold a card on file, and collect a deposit before the customer comes in.
  • No PCI fee, no statement fee, no batch fee, no setup fee, no contract and no cancellation fee. If we ever stop earning it, you walk.
