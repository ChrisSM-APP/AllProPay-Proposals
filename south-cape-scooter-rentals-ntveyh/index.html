Where We Are

You and Christopher built South Cape Scooter Rentals from the ground up, and two years in you are running it lean: rentals, repairs, deposits held on a card, and a lot of miles between deliveries while you scout the next location. Your Clover setup got you started, but right now your customers pay 3.99% on card sales and you pay around $43 every month in service charges for a system you have already outgrown.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Keep your deposit workflow exactly as it is. Hold the card at pickup, inspect at return, charge tolls, gas, or damage off the hold. Nothing about how you operate changes.
  • Lower the fee your customers pay. 3.5% instead of the 3.99% they pay today.
  • Cut the monthly cost. $25 all-in instead of about $43, with new equipment included.
  • Stay mobile while you are between locations. A wireless terminal plus Tap to Pay on your iPhone, so a forgotten machine never costs you a sale.
  • No contract. The business is moving and growing. You should not be locked into anything while it does.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Built for how you run today
Wireless terminal for the customers who want a machine, plus Tap to Pay on your iPhone for everyone else. Send invoices and payment links by text or email from the same app. It all runs on your hotspot, no shop required.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are between locations, the season swings from $10,000 card months down to $4,000, and you are not sure where the next shop will be. This setup does not care. It runs off a hotspot, lives on your phone, and scales up the day you sign a lease.

  • Start on Edge at 3.5%. If you ever prefer to absorb the fee yourself, we switch you to interchange-plus on the same account, no new application.
  • Recurring billing is ready when you want it, ideal for the off-season long-term rentals you are starting to see.
  • You mentioned you are not stuck on QuickBooks. When you are ready, we connect you with an accounting setup that integrates directly with your payments, so everything flows in one place.
  • When the new location opens: point of sale, inventory, and online payments are all things we add to the same account, and we walk you through each one.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your customers pay less. 3.5% instead of 3.99% on every card sale. The fee they see at checkout gets smaller, not bigger.
  • Your monthly cost drops by about 40%. $25 all-in instead of roughly $43, and that covers equipment, compliance, and support.
  • New equipment, nothing to carry. A wireless terminal for customers who want a machine, and Tap to Pay on the iPhone already in your pocket for everyone else.
