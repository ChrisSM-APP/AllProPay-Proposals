What This Means
1The checks were never free
QuickBooks bills 1% on every check and bank transfer, capped at $20. In July that was $75.50 across four payments you had every reason to think arrived clean. Moving a catering customer off a card and onto a check saves you the 2.99%, but it does not get you to zero. Nothing you can do inside QuickBooks gets you to zero.
2You are turning down the payment your customers want
You told me your catering customers would rather pay by card and that you push them toward check or Zelle. Today saying yes costs you 2.99%, so of course you steer them. On Edge saying yes costs you nothing. That means no more negotiating payment method on a $5,000 order, and no more waiting on a check to arrive.
3Your instinct on the truck was right, it just is not automated
On SpotOn you already pass the card fee to the customer, which is why that side costs the business almost nothing. Edge does exactly that, automatically, on both the truck and the catering invoices, in one portal connected to QuickBooks. Next-day funding, and you pick the time the day closes.
4At 4% the money comes back the other way
Post 4% on Edge and we pay you 0.25% of your card volume every month, plus a $100 bonus across your first six months. At $50,000 a month in card sales that is about $125 back to you monthly, at $75,000 closer to $188. Or run 3.5% and skip the rebate, which puts your customers below both SpotOn plans. Your card cost is $0 either way, so this is genuinely your choice to make.
