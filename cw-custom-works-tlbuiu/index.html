Adding 3% to card invoices was the right instinct. Here is the part worth checking. Your processor charges its percentage on the new, higher total, so a large share of what you collect goes straight back out the door. On Edge, a $1,000 job nets you $1,000 whether the customer pays by card or by bank transfer.

You do not have to take my word for it. Pull your HouseCall Pro fee report for last month and put what you collected in added fees next to what they charged you. That one comparison tells you whether this is worth your time better than anything I can write here.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You have not signed up for a box you cannot get out of. The account bends to how you decide to run things.

  • If you ever decide you would rather absorb the card fee yourself, we move you to flat interchange-plus pricing on the same account. No new application.
  • Big invoices can go out with bank transfer as the cheaper option, so the customer chooses and you keep more on the jobs where it matters most.
  • If you ever start taking payment in the truck or at a counter, we add a terminal or tap-to-pay on the phone to the same account.
  • No contract. If it is not working, you leave.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your cost to take a card goes to $0. A $1,000 job nets you $1,000.
  • Your card customers see the same 3% they see today. There is no new conversation with anyone you already bill.
  • The people paying by bank transfer or check pay the lower price, instead of carrying a charge that was never theirs.
  • Everything stays in HouseCall Pro. Same invoices, same payment links, same workflow, same records.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee.
