What We Are Seeing

Based on your volume, ticket profile, and card environment, three things stand out.

1. Blended Pricing at Scale

Blended rates are straightforward to read but tend to cost more at higher volumes because the pricing does not adjust for card type. Consumer debit, which is typically the cheapest card to process, gets charged the same blended rate as premium rewards cards. For a shop doing your volume, the difference between blended and interchange-plus pricing is often several thousand dollars a year.

We cannot put a firm number on this without seeing your statement, but the effective rate you described - roughly 2.7% - is consistent with what we typically see when there is meaningful room to clean up.

2. Manual Fee Pass-Through - Compliance Exposure

Adding a fee manually before running the card through your terminal is a common workaround, but it carries risk. Card brands have specific rules about how fees can be disclosed and applied. A manual process - separate from an automated, compliant program - may not meet those requirements.

We are not raising this to alarm you. Most shops running this setup are unaware of the exposure. An automated program handles disclosure, receipt language, and debit exclusion correctly from the start, which eliminates the risk entirely.

3. Equipment and Service Fees

Hardware rental and service fees on legacy terminal arrangements have increased industry-wide. These costs are worth accounting for in the total picture. Depending on your current equipment setup, there may be a straightforward path to better hardware at lower or no ongoing cost.

Working Capital + Equipment Financing

This is the part that did not come up before, and it matters.

AllProPay offers working capital and equipment financing tied directly to monthly processing volume. This is not a banking product. It does not touch your M&T relationship, your loan covenants, or any existing lending arrangements. The two are completely separate.

What This Looks Like for a Business at Your Volume

Merchants processing $1M+ annually typically qualify for meaningful capital options based on consistent monthly volume. The structure is designed around cash flow, not collateral.

  • Short-term working capital advances - tied to monthly processing, repaid as a percentage of daily volume. No fixed monthly payment.
  • Equipment financing - shop upgrades, tooling, lifts, diagnostic equipment. Structured over terms that match the useful life of the equipment.
  • Flexible repayment - repayment moves with your volume. Slower months mean smaller repayments. You are not locked into a fixed amount regardless of how business is running.

Approval and terms are based on processing history and volume. A 15-minute call is the right place to go through what makes sense for your specific situation - we do not quote terms without knowing the details.

If access to capital has been part of how you have thought about your banking relationship, knowing this option exists changes the picture. You can keep everything with M&T exactly as it is and still have a capital line tied to your processing volume through AllProPay.
