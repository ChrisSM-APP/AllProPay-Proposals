What is not working

Tap and Amex have been off your POS terminals for over four months

You told us that despite repeated requests over the last four-plus months, eBiz has not enabled contactless or Amex acceptance on your Move 5000 POS terminals. That has a real cost:

  • Every tap-preferring customer at the counter gets slowed down or turned away from how they want to pay.
  • No Amex at the counter means a card your customers carry simply does not work for lube sales, race fuel, or in-person bill payments.
  • The terminals are rented through Elavon, so you are paying every month for equipment that is not doing the full job.

This is not a pricing problem. It is a service problem. Four months of unanswered requests is the issue we want to solve first.

What the new POS terminals will do

From our call, the new terminals at the branch locations need to do four things. This is the spec we are building to:

  • Accept Visa, Mastercard, and Amex at the counter, all three, from day one.
  • Tap and contactless enabled out of the box, not four months later.
  • An invoice-number prompt at checkout so every POS sale ties back to a sales invoice. That is what lets your team reconcile counter transactions against bank deposits cleanly.
  • Built for branch counter use for lube sales, race fuel purchases, and in-person bill payments.

Elavon uses ERR (Enhanced Recover Reduced) pricing. Your 0.15% discount rate does not include the network assessment fees that Elavon charges separately as a pass-through on the same transactions.
