What We Found

Interchange Tier Mismatch

Law firms processing large-ticket B2B invoices typically see a high proportion of commercial and corporate card transactions. These transactions qualify for lower interchange rates when processed with Level 2/3 data (client reference numbers, tax amounts, invoice details). At $200,000/month, an unoptimized interchange setup can cost $300 to $600 per month more than necessary. North's default configuration does not automatically optimize for this - it passes the cost to you.

Junk Fee Stack

North / NAB is well known for layering fees above the processing rate. These are not interchange costs - they are North's own charges added to your statement. Common examples include PCI compliance fees ($15-30/month), regulatory compliance fees ($15-25/month), batch fees, auth surcharges above standard, statement fees, and service fees. At your volume, these typically add $75 to $200/month on top of the processing rate - fees that disappear when you move to a clean-structure processor.

No Direct Account Management

North operates a shared call center model. When you have a question, a dispute, or a billing concern, you are calling a 1-800 number. For a firm processing $200,000 per month, that is not acceptable. You should have a named account manager who knows your setup and picks up the phone. This is a standard part of what AllProPay provides - it is not an upgrade.

What Stays the Same

Authorize.net - No Change

Authorize.net remains your gateway. You log in the same way. The virtual terminal interface is identical. Batch processing, reporting, and transaction history all remain within Authorize.net. Your team does not learn a new system.

QuickBooks Online Integration - No Change

The Authorize.net to QuickBooks Online sync continues without interruption. The integration is at the gateway level, not the processor level. Switching the backend processor has no effect on it.

Trust Accounting and Compliance - No Change

Your current payment structure - including any client trust account handling - carries over. We review the compliance setup before migrating anything and confirm it is intact before you process a single transaction under the new structure.

What does change: the backend processor (invisible to your daily operation), the fee structure (lower), and the support model (direct contact instead of a call center).
