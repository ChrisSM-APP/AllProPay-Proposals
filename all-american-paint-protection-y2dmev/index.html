The Flexibility You Asked For

You mentioned wanting to see it both ways: one version where you absorb the card cost, one where you pass it on. The pricing above is the absorb version. The other is AllProPay Edge, our dual pricing program: the posted price becomes the card price, cash pays list, and your cost on card sales drops to about $0 plus the same $25 monthly. At your volume that moves the entire processing bill, estimated at tens of thousands a year, off your books and onto the card-paying customer. Both run on the same account, so you can start on one and switch whenever you want, no new application and no new equipment.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today.
  • Switch to dual pricing whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run a few months on IC+, see the actual cost on your statement, and decide if you want to move it to the customer. No penalty either way.
