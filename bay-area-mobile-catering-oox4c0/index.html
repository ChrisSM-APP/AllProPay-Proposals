Where We Are

Let me start with the honest part. You told me on our first call that you do not pay processing fees because you are on SpotOn, and having gone through July, you are right. On the truck you post 4% to the card-paying customer and your own cost is zero. I am not going to sit here and tell you I can beat zero. What I can do is pay you for that same volume, and fix the side of your business nobody has looked at.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • The truck is already handled. You post 4%, your customers carry it, your cost is $0. On Edge that stays exactly as it is, same 4%, same checkout, same experience for every customer. Nothing to relearn and nothing for them to notice.
  • What changes on the truck is that you get paid for it. SpotOn sends you nothing. We send back 0.25% of your card volume every month, plus $100 across your first six.
  • Catering is the part that is actually costing you. QuickBooks charged 2.99% on the two card payments in July and you absorbed all of it.
  • The four check payments were not free either. QuickBooks takes 1% of every check, capped at $20. That was $75.50 in July on money you had every reason to think arrived clean.
  • All in, catering cost you $157.87 on $14,025 of invoices. It is the only line in your business where you personally pay to accept a payment, and it is the one thing you cannot fix inside QuickBooks at any price.

You told me your catering customers would rather pay by card and that you push them toward check or Zelle instead. Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.
