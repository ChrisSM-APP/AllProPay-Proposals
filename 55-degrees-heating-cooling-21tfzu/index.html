The Problem with Stripe for Field Service

Stripe is built for e-commerce, not HVAC

Stripe was designed for online stores and SaaS companies. It is not optimized for field service businesses. You are paying e-commerce rates on in-person and invoice-based work -- and there is no reason to.

No leverage, no relationship

With Stripe, you are a line item. There is no rep to call, no one to advocate for you, and no ability to negotiate. As your volume grows, your fees grow with it at the same flat rate.

The integration is the lock-in

The reason most HouseCall Pro users stay on Stripe longer than they should is that it feels like changing it would be complicated. It is not -- especially when we handle the switchover for you.
