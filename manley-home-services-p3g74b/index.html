Where We Are

This is the written version of what we walked through on the phone, in one page you can pass along to whoever helps you with the numbers. The headline is simple, and it is the part that is easy to miss on a quick call:

  • You keep Jobber exactly as it is. You do not switch software, you do not learn a new system, and nothing changes for you or your customers. You keep paying your normal Jobber subscription.
  • We replace Stripe behind the scenes. Jobber now allows outside processors to run through its built-in Authorize.net connection. We take over from Stripe on the back end, at a lower rate, while everything stays inside Jobber.
  • This is Jobber-sanctioned, not a workaround. Jobber opened this up the same way QuickBooks lets you bring your own processor. It is on Jobber's own support page, not a third-party bolt-on.
  • A lower rate works in your favor either way. You said you pass the fee to the customer. A lower rate means a smaller fee they see, so fewer of them reach for cash. And on the jobs where you absorb it, you keep more.

Numbers below reflect what was shared on the call. One recent Jobber or Stripe statement replaces the estimate with your exact rate, so the comparison is apples to apples and not a guess.

Why This Is the Right Move
  • You do not have to leave Jobber. The most common worry is "do I have to switch software." You do not. Jobber stays exactly as it is, and Jobber itself allows this.
  • The fee your customers see gets smaller. You already pass it on. When the rate is lower, the line on their bill is lower, so fewer customers balk and ask to pay cash.
  • Stripe is the expensive default, not the only option. Jobber uses Stripe out of the box, and Stripe's flat rate is built for convenience, not for volume like yours. At $25k a month, that gap is real money.
  • There is no reason not to check the numbers. No contract, no cancellation fee. Send one statement, see the exact comparison, decide from there.

You said it yourself: you do not believe in processing fees, and you would rather a customer just pay. The honest fix is not to hide the fee, it is to make it smaller. A lower rate inside the same Jobber you already use does exactly that, and it keeps more in your pocket on the jobs you cover yourself.
