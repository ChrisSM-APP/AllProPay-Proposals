Executive Summary

Wash Nash is running a surcharge model -- the right call. Customers cover the processing fee, so the cost of payment processing comes straight out of what those surcharges bring in. The problem is the rate: Stripe's standard bundled pricing was built for merchants who absorb their own fees, not for surcharge accounts. You are paying a premium rate for a service you are already recovering from customers.

On roughly $25,000 in monthly volume, that adds up to about $775 going to Stripe every month -- $9,300 per year. Switching to IC Plus pricing via AllProPay brings that down by an estimated $179/month, with zero change to how you run Jobber.

What We Found

Stripe Standard Rate on a Surcharge Model

Stripe's 3.00% + $0.30/transaction is a bundled flat rate priced to cover Stripe's costs across all card types. You pay that blended premium whether your customers use a basic debit card or a premium rewards card.

IC Plus pricing strips that out. You pay the actual interchange rate for each card type, plus a fixed 0.50% markup and $0.10 per transaction. On a typical field service portfolio, the blended effective rate comes out lower than Stripe's flat bundled rate every month.
