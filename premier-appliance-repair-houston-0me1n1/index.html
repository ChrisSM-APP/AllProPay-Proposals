I am not going to tell you I found you savings, because you already took the fee off your own back. What I found is three things your current setup is costing you that are not showing up as a line item. You are doing the 4% math by hand on every single invoice, across Chase and Stripe and ShopMonkey, usually while you are driving. Your customers are paying 4% when the program runs at 3.5%, so they are paying more than they need to. And a flat 4% added on top is not something the card brands allow: a surcharge is capped at the lesser of 3% or your actual cost, it has to be registered with the networks, and it can never be applied to a debit card. Set up as dual pricing instead of an add-on fee, every one of those problems goes away and your cost stays exactly where you put it, at zero.

  • Nothing is locked. If we stop earning it, you walk, and you take your customers and your history with you.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • You stop doing the 4% math by hand. It applies itself on the invoice, every time, including the ones you write while you are driving.
  • Your customers pay 3.5% instead of 4%, which is roughly $900 a year that stays with the people who keep calling you back.
