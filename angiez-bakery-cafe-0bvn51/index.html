The "Free Terminal" Question

You were pitched a free terminal. It is worth being clear about how that works, because "free" hardware is almost always paid for somewhere else, usually in the rate.

At a typical monthly volume in the range of $40,000 to $50,000 across your two shops, here is the difference in simple terms:

  • A flat $40 terminal is a known, fixed cost. It is the same line on your statement every month, forever.
  • A "free" terminal recovered through the rate rises every time your sales rise. A higher per-transaction rate on $50,000 of volume can quietly cost more than the rental it replaced, and it never stops.
  • The real comparison is total monthly cost, not the hardware line. When you look at processing plus equipment together, the flat-fee setup is usually the cheaper one at your volume.
When the statement comes in, we will lay your current Nuvei cost next to this setup line by line so you can see the all-in difference, not just the rate headline.
