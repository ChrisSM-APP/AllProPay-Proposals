Where We Are

Greg, here is the plan we talked through, in one place. Let me say the most important part first: you are not overpaying on fees. Running on Zelle and Venmo keeps your hard cost at zero, and that is a real advantage worth protecting. This is not a pitch to start paying fees you do not pay today.

The problem is not price. The problem is that everything runs through you. There is no card checkout on the Swing LabPro site, your staff cannot take or book payments on their own, and peer-to-peer apps were never built to run a business on. The goal here is simple: give Swing LabPro a real payment system your team can run without you, and do it without giving up your near-zero cost.

You told me what matters most. The setup is built around it:

  • Keep my cost at or near zero. No starting to pay fees you do not pay now.
  • Card payments built into the website. Customers book and pay online automatically, no manual chasing.
  • My staff can run it. Each agent gets their own login to book and process, so you stop being the bottleneck.
  • No long contracts. Month to month, cancel anytime, no termination penalty.
What You Get
A real gateway on your site
A proper payment gateway (Fluidpay or Authorize.net) built into the Swing LabPro site. Clean API, stable webhooks, plugs straight into your Hostinger and N8N setup. Customers check out and book without you touching it.
Logins for your whole team
Each staff member gets their own access to book sessions and run payments on their own. Role-based permissions, full reporting, and a clean record of who did what. You stop being the single point everything waits on.
Your cost stays at zero
A built-in cash discount moves the card fee to the paying customer, so your processing bill stays at or near zero, same as today. And you gain real dispute and chargeback handling that Zelle and Venmo do not give a business.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are not boxing yourself in by starting. The two models live on the same account, so moving between them is a setting, not a re-install.

  • Run cash discount from day one. Your cost stays at zero and the customer covers the card fee, the same way a cash price and a card price differ today.
  • Switch to interchange-plus whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same logins. Only the fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. No penalty either way. You decide what fits Swing LabPro once it is live and you can see it running.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Zelle and Venmo were not built for business. Running company revenue through personal-style apps risks holds and account freezes, and their terms do not cover business use the way a real merchant account does.
  • No dispute protection today. If a customer disputes a Zelle or Venmo payment, you are mostly on your own. A real merchant account gives you a proper chargeback and dispute process.
  • You are the bottleneck. Every booking and payment waits on you. Staff logins and an automated checkout take that off your plate.
  • Card customers convert better. A clean card checkout on your site captures the people who will not stop to open a payment app, and it runs around the clock.
  • Nothing to lose on cost. The cash discount model keeps your processing at zero, so you modernize without adding an expense.

At the $10,000 to $19,000 a month you are running, this is less about saving a few dollars and more about getting your time back and letting the business grow without you in the middle of every transaction.
