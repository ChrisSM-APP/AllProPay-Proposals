What You Get
Nothing about your day changes
You told me the shop is busy and you do not want your life turned upside down. Fair. So the swap is a terminal swap. A smart terminal goes out overnight, it takes a tap the same way your Square reader does, and your customers will not notice a thing. No new software to learn and no retraining for whoever is on the counter.
True cost instead of a flat rate
Square charges you one flat price on every card. A debit card, a basic Visa, and a premium rewards card all cost Square very different amounts to run, but you pay the same on all three. On true-cost pricing you pay interchange, the real cost the card networks set, plus a flat 0.50 percent and 8 cents a transaction. That is the entire markup, and everything Square was keeping on the cheap cards stays with you.
A rate that can actually move
Square's flat rate is published and it is the same for everybody, so it never comes down no matter how much you grow. At $50,000 to $100,000 a month you are well past the point where a flat rate is doing you any favors. On true-cost pricing your cost follows your card mix, and you have a named rep who picks up the phone, sets the account up the way you want it, and handles any chargeback paperwork with you.
