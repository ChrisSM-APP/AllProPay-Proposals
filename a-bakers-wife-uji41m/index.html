What We Found

1. Clover's tiered pricing absorbs $33,000 - $38,000 per year at this volume

Clover's standard tiered model charges the same bundled rate regardless of whether a customer pays with a low-cost debit card or a high-cost rewards card. For a bakery doing $100,000 per month, that bundled rate typically lands between 2.8% and 3.2% effective -- meaning $2,800 to $3,200 every month is leaving the business as processing costs.

AllProPay's interchange-plus program passes through the actual card cost plus a fixed markup. For a bakery with a typical card mix (debit-heavy, card-present), the effective rate typically runs 2.1% to 2.5%. At $100,000 per month, that difference is worth $300 to $1,100 per month -- or $3,600 to $13,200 per year. A statement will confirm exactly where it lands for A Bakers Wife.

2. Two systems -- Shopify and Clover -- means manual work every day

Running online orders through Shopify and in-store transactions through Clover means the bakery's sales data lives in two completely separate places. Inventory updates do not sync automatically. End-of-day reporting requires pulling from both systems. Any discrepancy between what sold online and what is physically in stock has to be caught and corrected by hand.

AllProPay's preferred POS -- EPOSnow -- integrates directly with Shopify. Online and in-store sales flow into a single dashboard in real time. Inventory updates automatically when an item sells through either channel. Reports pull from one place. The reconciliation work that currently falls on your team goes away.
