The Flexibility You Asked For

You originally looked at a customer-paid model (cash discount / dual pricing) so that processing cost goes to zero on your side. We built this account so you can flip to that program later without changing anything in QuickBooks and without re-applying.

  • Start on interchange-plus now. Clean, transparent, and lower than what you pay today. You keep absorbing the cost, your customers see no service fee on their invoice.
  • Switch to dual pricing whenever you want. Same account, same gateway, same QuickBooks workflow. The fee structure changes, the plumbing does not.
  • Try one, then the other. Run a few months on IC+, see the actual cost on your statement, and decide if you want to move it to the customer. No penalty either way.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • QuickBooks freeze risk goes away. You are underwritten as an electrical contractor running large tickets. That is the whole point.
  • Your cost on large tickets drops the most. A $22K invoice on QuickBooks at roughly 5 percent costs you about $1,100. On IC+ at around 3 percent blended, that same invoice runs closer to $660. The bigger the ticket, the bigger the gap.
  • AMEX stops being the most expensive card. OptBlue puts AMEX in line with Visa and Mastercard pricing.

  • One point of contact. You call Tammy. Not a 1-800 queue.
