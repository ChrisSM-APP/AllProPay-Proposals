Where We Are

Here is what you told Kurt and me, so you and Jeremy can check I was listening. The plan is simple: keep running deals the way you do, take the deposit the second the buyer says yes, and stop paying Square's top tier every time a card gets typed in.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • About 95% of your deals happen remotely. The buyer puts a deposit down by card, the balance comes by wire, and you ship the car.
  • Deposits are $500 on cars under $50,000 and $1,000 over. Most of them get keyed in over the phone so you can mark the car sale pending right away. Time kills deals.
  • Consignors pay in person when they drop a car off, and cars past 90 days carry a $150 monthly storage charge, all through Square today.
  • You have paid Square about $30,000 in card fees so far this year and absorbed all of it as a cost of doing business.
  • You keep card deposits at $1,000 because of past chargebacks, even when a buyer wants to put more down.
What You Get
One price, however the card comes in
Square charges you three different rates: 2.6% plus 15 cents in person, 3.3% plus 30 cents on an invoice, and 3.5% plus 15 cents when you key the card in. With me every one of them is interchange, the true cost set by Visa and Mastercard, plus a flat 0.5% and 8 cents. Keying a card in over the phone does not cost you extra. A card terminal for the counter is included free.
Deposits in minutes, not checks in the mail
Text the buyer a payment link while you are still on the phone. They pay by card or straight from their bank account, you get a message the second it clears, and you mark the car sale pending. A bank payment costs you 0.75%, capped at $10, so the buyer who would have mailed a check has a faster option that costs you almost nothing.
Deposits that hold up
Your non-refundable deposit terms go out with the payment link, so the buyer agrees to them before they pay. If someone tries to reverse a deposit, you call me, not a chat queue, and I work the paperwork with you. No contract and no cancellation fee.

Want It Lower Still? Your Other Options

You told me you would rather keep absorbing the card fee for now, so the plan starts with the lower rate above and nothing your buyers see. Two more options sit on the same account, and you can switch either one on whenever you and Jeremy want. AllProPay Edge: buyers who pay with a credit card cover the card cost at 3.5%, so a $500 deposit becomes $517.50 and your cost on that sale is $0. Debit cards are never charged extra and cost you 0.50% plus 10 cents. Bank transfer by link: the buyer pays from their bank account and it costs you 0.75%, capped at $10.
