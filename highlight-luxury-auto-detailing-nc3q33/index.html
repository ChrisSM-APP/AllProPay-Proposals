Where We Are

When we spoke you told me the business runs mostly on cash, and the card payments you do take run through your Wix site. You also said card processing does not cost you anything today, just the monthly Wix subscription. Here is the part worth a closer look: Wix does take a processing fee, about 2.9% plus 30 cents on every card sale. It comes out of your deposit before the money reaches your account, so you never get a separate bill for it. My goal is simple. Keep your Wix setup exactly as it is, and get that card cost as close to zero as possible.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Keep your Wix site and checkout exactly as they work today. We integrate with your setup, you do not rebuild anything.
  • Most of your business stays cash at list price. Nothing about that changes.
  • Lower the cost on the card payments that do come through, ideally to zero.
  • Add a card reader, a payment gateway, or a pay-by-link only if and when you actually need it. No contract either way.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
