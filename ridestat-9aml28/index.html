The Situation

RideStat books rides by phone, online, and through facility coordinators. Most cards are keyed in by hand, which is exactly the kind of transaction Square charges the most for: 3.5% + $0.15 per keyed card. On top of that, Square is a flat-rate aggregator. There is no way to negotiate, no real invoicing for facility AR, no built-in ACH, and funds can be held without warning if your deposit pattern shifts.

AllProPay moves you off the flat rate and onto a real merchant account with interchange-plus pricing. You pay only the true wholesale cost of the card plus a small, fixed markup. That is where the savings live.
