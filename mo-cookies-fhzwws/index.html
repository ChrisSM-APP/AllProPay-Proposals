Square is simple, and simple is expensive on a bakery ticket. Square's published in-store rate is 2.6 percent plus 10 cents on every tap, and on a $6 cookie sale that 10 cents alone is 1.7 percent, so the real cost on that sale is about 4.3 percent. On $10,000 a month that is roughly $310 to $385 out of your deposits, and the smaller your average ticket, the closer you sit to the top of that range. On Edge the card-paying customer covers about 24 cents on that same $6 sale, shown on the terminal before they tap, and you keep the $6. Your cost on the sale is $0. The whole account is $25 a month.
