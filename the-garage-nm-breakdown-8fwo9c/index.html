What This Means

1. You were quoted 2.2%. July says 2.48%.

Nobody misled you; 2.2% is roughly what a typical card mix costs on this pricing. Yours is not typical. Business cards, fleet cards and purchasing cards were 18% of your July volume, premium rewards cards another 29%, and AMEX 11% at 3.05%. Those are the most expensive cards the networks publish, and they cost you 2.09% before Wells Fargo added a cent. The rate you were quoted was never the number on the statement.

2. Wells Fargo keeps 0.38%. I take it to 0.21%, all in.

Your processor's layer is 0.358% on every brand plus $60.25 a month in account fees. Mine is 0.20% plus 5 cents a transaction plus $25, with the PCI, statement and system fees gone and PCI compliance handled as part of the service. That is 44% less to your processor, about $450 a month on July's volume, and every line of it is checkable against the statement in your hand. Be clear about the size of it, though: 44% off a small number is still a small number. If a competitor tells you they can cut your rate dramatically, ask what their markup is and compare it to 0.358%.
