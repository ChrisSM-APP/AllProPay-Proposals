The Short Version

You told us the same thing we hear from a lot of QuickBooks Online users: the software dropped the ball on a few things that used to be automatic on QuickBooks Desktop. You are checking every invoice before it goes out so the sales tax does not come out wrong, and you are manually adding the 3% card fee on top each time. With just you and your wife running the business, that is real time spent every week.

The recommendation here is not a software switch. QuickBooks stays. You add one plug-in called Hyfin that sits inside your QuickBooks. It applies the card fee automatically, syncs the sales tax automatically, and reconciles the invoice as paid the moment your customer pays. The processing in the background runs on a real card processor instead of Intuit Payments, which is what unlocks next-day funding and the dual-pricing model.

The end result: you keep 100% of the invoice amount, you stop double-checking sales tax on every invoice, and the only flat monthly cost is the $100 integration fee. No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee.
