What This Means
1Tiered pricing is the fixable part.
On Visa and Mastercard you are billed a low "qualified" rate, then surcharged when a card does not qualify. In June that surcharge was $628.62 ($570.64 Visa + $57.98 Mastercard). Interchange-plus has no qualified / non-qualified buckets at all, so those downgrade penalties simply do not exist. This is where the surest savings live.
2AMEX is the honest ceiling.
At 57% of your volume and about 3.0%, AMEX is already near market. It is expensive because AMEX makes it expensive, not because of your processor. On our program the AMEX win is a tighter acquirer markup and one clean bill, not a big rate cut. We would rather tell you that than sell you a number we cannot hit.
3The all-in target is 2.5% to 2.8%.
Clean up the Visa/Mastercard tiers, tighten the AMEX markup, and drop the junk statement fee, and a setup like yours blends closer to 2.5% to 2.8%. On your June volume that is an estimated $250 to $500 a month back, roughly $3,000 to $6,000 a year. Estimated from this one statement, not guaranteed. Your card mix month to month sets the exact figure.
