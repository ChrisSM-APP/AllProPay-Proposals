Backed and flexible

The Flexibility You Asked For

You are seven months in and the fleet work is only starting, so the account is built to not get in the way of where this goes.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you two ever decide you would rather absorb the card cost yourselves, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account, with no reapplication and no new paperwork.
  • The ACH lane turns on when the fleet contracts do. A large fleet invoice does not have to run on a card at all.
  • A second phone, a second truck, or Bill putting on a hand costs nothing extra. The $25 is the account, not the seat.
