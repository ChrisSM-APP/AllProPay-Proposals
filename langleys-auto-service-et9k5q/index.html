Where We Are

When we talked, you told me something that matters more than any rate quote. You are not using Clover for inventory, for parts, or for invoicing. You are using it to take cards. That means you are carrying the cost of a system built for a very different kind of business, and paying processing on top of it. You also told me you are stretched thin right now with the shop and with hiring, so whatever we do has to be simple and it cannot create work for you.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • The counter workflow stays exactly the same. Swipe, chip, tap, and the occasional key-in when somebody pays over the phone.
  • The processing cost comes off the business. That was the whole point of the conversation.
  • One flat monthly cost, no contract, and no equipment to buy. Your Clover hardware is paid off, so there is nothing to unwind.
  • Simple enough that you and your wife can both read it and decide without a long meeting.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash and check customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Same counter, same workflow
A standalone terminal replaces the Clover at the counter. Swipe, chip, and tap all work the way they do today, and it handles a phone key-in when a customer pays that way. The adjustment is applied automatically, so nobody at the front desk has to do math or defend a policy at the counter.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You have not signed anything and you should not have to guess. Here is how the account moves if your situation changes.

  • Start on Edge with $0 card cost to the business. If you and your wife ever decide you would rather absorb the fee yourselves, I move you to straight interchange-plus on the same account. No new application, no new equipment.
  • Give it 30 days. There is no contract and no cancellation fee, so if it is not working the way I described it, you walk and it costs you nothing.
  • If you ever do want a system that runs parts and invoicing, we can talk about it then. You told me you do not use one, so I am not selling you one now.
