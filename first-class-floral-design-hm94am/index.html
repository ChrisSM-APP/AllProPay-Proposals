Where We Are

You run First Class Floral on Square today: the tablet terminal at the counter on East Main, and a Square-hosted website that carries most of your orders. On the phone last week you were straight with us. You cover the card fees yourself, you claim them back at tax time, and Square has treated you fairly. So this is not a page telling you your processor is bad. It is two things: take the card fee off your counter, and give you one person to call by name when a holiday week gets loud.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • Nothing changes about how a customer pays at the counter. Tap, dip, or swipe, the same as the tablet does today.
  • Your website stays exactly where it is. It is built on Square, so it stays on Square, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.
  • No contract and no cancellation fee, so if we stop earning the account you walk.
  • One person on the account. My direct line, not a help center queue.
What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
On the Edge program the card fee moves to the card-paying customer as a small, clearly labeled adjustment. Cash and check customers pay your listed price. On a card sale, your business keeps effectively all of it.
Same counter, same workflow
The terminal arrives programmed and ready to plug in. Your customers tap, dip, or swipe the way they do now, and both the card price and the cash price show on the screen before they pay. Taking an order over the counter does not change.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You said you would rather not be locked into anything, so the account is built to move with you.

  • Start on Edge with no card cost to the business. If you ever decide you would rather absorb the fee yourself again, we move you onto AllProPay Rate on the same account with no new application.
  • Run it by season if you want. Some shops keep the card price on all year, others switch it on for the two big holiday weeks and take it back off after.
  • If you ever rebuild the website off Square, we can carry the online side too. Until then it stays where it is.
Why This Is the Right Move
  • Your card cost at the counter goes to $0. The card-paying customer covers the processing, and it is disclosed on the screen before they pay.
  • You get my direct line. The same person for pricing, equipment, deposits, and the odd Saturday problem, instead of a help center and a ticket number.
  • Your two big weeks get handled up front. Before February I tell our risk team what a Valentine's week looks like for a florist your size, so a jump in volume is expected rather than questioned.
  • Nothing changes at the counter. The terminal arrives programmed, and your customers tap, dip, or swipe exactly as they do now.
  • One thing worth checking before we talk: orders that come in through your website are charged a processing fee too, and the published online rate runs higher than the in-person one. Pull last month's deposits and I will go through that line with you, whether or not you move anything.
  • No contract, no setup fee, no cancellation fee, no PCI fee, and no statement fee. If we stop earning it, you leave.

On a $75 arrangement paid by card, the processing comes off your side of the ticket entirely, and the customer who pays cash pays about $3 less than the card price. Put that against a Valentine's week and it stops being small.
