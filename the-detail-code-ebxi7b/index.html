Where We Are

Aaron, thanks for taking the call this morning between customers. You asked for everything I walked you through in writing so you and your partner can look at it together, so here it is. Where you are today: QuickBooks does your books and your invoicing, most customers pay by card or cash against an invoice, tickets run $300 to $400, and you work out the card fee and add it to each invoice yourself. That works, but it is you doing the processor's job with a calculator. This setup keeps QuickBooks exactly where it is and takes the math, and the cost, off your plate.

You told us what mattered most. We built the setup around it:

  • QuickBooks stays. Your books and your customer list do not move. Every sale posts to QuickBooks as paid, terminal or invoice.

What You Get
Zero-cost card processing
Every card sale shows two prices before the customer pays: the card price and the cash price. The cash price is your price today. The card price carries the fee, the same amount you add by hand now, except the invoice and the terminal print it for you. Pay by card and the customer pays the card price and you keep your full price. Pay cash and they pay the cash price. Either way your cost on the sale is $0, and nobody pulled out a calculator.
Built for the shop and the driveway
A wireless terminal for the shop, tap to pay on your phone for the jobs you do on site, and invoices by text or email for anyone who pays after the fact. Whenever a customer is standing in front of you, tap the card. A card tapped in person carries much stronger protection against a dispute than an invoice paid after the work is done, and every one of those sales posts to QuickBooks the same way.
Backed and flexible
Direct underwriting, next-day funding, no contract and no cancellation fee. If you ever want to absorb the fee yourself instead, we move you to flat interchange-plus on the same account.

What I Said on the Call, in Writing

You said the recap only helps if everything from the phone is on paper. These are the three things I spent the most time on.

If a customer disputes a charge

The disputed amount is held, not refunded on its own. Our team calls you, you send over what you have (the invoice, the photos, the sign-off), and we take it up with the card issuer on your behalf. Document every job the way you already do and you are in a strong position. Tap the card in person whenever you can and you are in the strongest one.

When you get paid

Next business day, standard. A card run on Monday is in your account Tuesday, Tuesday lands Wednesday, and so on. No holds and no waiting on a schedule. The deposit shows up in QuickBooks against the invoices it paid.

What stays the same

QuickBooks is still where your books live and where you look things up. Your prices do not change; the cash price is the price you charge today. Your customers pay the way they pay now, card or cash, and the card customers see the card price before they pay it, the same way they do at more and more shops.

The Flexibility You Asked For

You told me you are open either way on the fee. The program is built so that stays your call, job by job, without a new application.

  • Turn it off for anyone you choose. A repeat customer, a neighbor, a fleet account you want to look after: one tap and that sale runs at the cash price on a card.
  • If you and your partner decide you would rather absorb the fee across the board, we move you to AllProPay Rate, interchange plus a flat markup, on the same account. It is on this page so you know it is real.
  • Nothing gets switched off until a real sale has run through the new setup. QuickBooks Payments stays live until you have watched it work on one of your own invoices.
